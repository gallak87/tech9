- **Asterion Orbital.** Start scouting around **1,040–1,070 m**, just before the fixed Limb lattice ends at about **1,080 m**. Hold past **1,096 m** into the moving section. Vary the live lead-in between takes so a moving opening widens right in front of Needle as it leaves the fixed span. Spend most of the shot in the animated section. Check the opening against the rail or cross-ribs at normal playback speed so the movement reads in steady chase. Keep rivals and colored wakes in frame where they fit around the road action.
- **Nacre Tempest.** Rehearse the full-field approach from the continuous review with Shrike. Hold the close chase view after the pass so the return fire, boost and nearby ships stay clear. Press **C** when the Storm Leap ramp comes into view on the final approach, aiming for about **one second before takeoff**. Let the vista settle over the leap with the pack in frame. Hold through touchdown and runout at native speed. Keep the pass, recovery of speed and jump in one take.

- **Track Workshop.** Start on a selected road point in a three-quarter view, with the curved Roll handle and both road edges visible. Drag the handle left or right and hold the entire gesture in one steady shot. Let the deck visibly bank and the signed Roll readout update before releasing. Use a point clear of the takeoff and receiving deck. Keep the drag and the jump edit on the same Nightweir draft, and trim the rebuild and load waits before the native test drive.
