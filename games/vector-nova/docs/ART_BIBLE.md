# Art direction

## Shared principles

The whole world and the raceway must share an identity: surface, edge silhouette, underside/supports, ordinary connectors, foreground, middle distance, sky and light. A strong hero vista cannot compensate for unfinished stretches.

Engage early and spread climbs, drops, banks, corkscrews and inversions through the lap, with readable recovery space. Do not stretch flat straights to meet a length target or cluster every dramatic feature in one short sequence.

Readability order: upcoming road/corner, craft/propulsion, gameplay feedback, decoration. Use real geometry, meaningful large/medium/small forms, coherent material response and grounded construction. Avoid bloom hiding form, screenshots posing as scenery, palette-swap levels and detail that vanishes at driving speed.

High/low presets retain the same world and navigation cues. Reduced motion restrains flashes and environmental movement. Use generated imagery only for appropriate asset/material roles, with exact provenance; preserve editable sources and licences.

## UI and feedback

Warm off-white/graphite, technical numerals, vermilion active accents, generous spacing and real controls. Keep telemetry and notices away from the craft, upcoming road and touch controls. Moderate chase FOV, no default camera shake, and road-local camera clearance.

Shared immediate devices use manufactured amber-white Vector strips and symbol-marked teal Aegis hardware. Show collection, active effect, hit and expiry with form/timing as well as color. Ship feedback stays subordinate to the silhouette.

Each world needs its own construction and material identity. Do not apply Halcyon asphalt or Asterion U frames as a universal template.
