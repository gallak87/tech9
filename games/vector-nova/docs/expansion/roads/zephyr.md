## Investigate

Inspect the apparent weave size and directional light response at chase distance. Determine whether the surface reads as fitted structural composite with a coating, and whether a curtain's wet region enriches that material without erasing it. Better mapping, material channels or highlights may matter more than a larger weave image. Preserve the dry/wet distinction and the warmth of the surrounding architecture.

## Keep and check

Compare a dry connector, the shaded nave wall ride, a wet curtain approach and its receiving shelf, plus the climbing spiral. Inspect weave aliasing, repetition through banks and abrupt wet boundaries in motion. Keep the disjoint running bands free of overlaps, and preserve curtain footprints, supported/gap intervals, guidance and exposure timing. Do not turn the whole lap wet or change the falling belfry event. Run relevant road/curtain checks alongside High/Low runtime verification.
