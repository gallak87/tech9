## Outcome

Create **Saffron Wide**, a separately selectable `saffron-wide` variant with a **40 m full driving surface throughout**, increased from the original 32 m (+25%). Preserve the original `saffron` as a working comparison. The complete circuit should feel roomier while retaining the existing fossil desert, glass construction, skull descent, rib enclosure, two vertebral gaps, optional dune skip, and living grazer.

Use the original route as the exact starting geometry: retain centerline coordinates, physical arc length, elevation, bank/roll, section and checkpoint distances, and jump takeoff/receiving intervals. Width and clearance metadata must change together. Do not widen just selected sections or leave narrow connectors. Natural authored gaps remain gaps. If an unavoidable geometric conflict would require altering the route or reducing the agreed width, present that specific conflict to the user rather than silently changing the brief.

Keep all player/rival ship sizes, handling profiles, 0.3 steering assistance, grounded/airborne physics, race rules, and grazer/event timing. Start with the existing chase/flight camera. Small variant-local framing corrections are permitted only if widened construction obstructs required driving/landing views; describe them in the handoff.

There are no separate art-polish assignments in this proof. Existing roadmap requests for deeper erosion, creature material work, richer sandfalls, or general performance optimization remain separate. Rework materials or affected assets only as needed to preserve their current quality after widening. Do not build a general expansion/deformation engine.

## Implementation sequence

1. **Establish the reference.** Record the source revision and any unrelated edits. Inspect all named sections in the original, the skull interior, each jump, and important existing fixes. Use deterministic scenarios and a small set of matching desktop driving/free-camera views; evidence belongs in `.experiments/expansion/saffron-wide/`. Do not run a normal-speed automated full lap.
2. **Create the complete variant.** Add `src/levels/saffron-wide/` with its own metadata/descriptor and local implementation. Keep the original code, behavior, exports, and tests intact. Reuse unchanged GLBs/textures from the original asset folder. Put genuinely modified sources/exports under `assets/levels/saffron-wide/` and `public/assets/levels/saffron-wide/`; ensure copied generators cannot overwrite the originals. Give the variant distinct track, geometry, runtime/collision rules, and replay identity, including the copied runtime's track-id guard. Preserve production/shape support, shared racing, free camera, and jump playtest integration.
3. **Rebuild the entire road.** Set the physical driving width to 40 m and render the matching surface (±20 m around its centerline). Adapt the existing glass cross-section, underside, toe boundaries, baffles, seams, fixtures, end faces, supports, and launch/receiving decks. Preserve material response, sensible thicknesses, and texture detail. Update grounded bounds, jump half-widths, optional-branch lateral limits, coverage, collision solids, and relevant clearance data at the same time. Remove superseded narrow geometry and colliders from the variant once replacements exist; do not leave a hidden 32 m physical roadway.
4. **Refit the adjacent world.** Keep existing scenery initially as a placement reference. Make skull openings, Ribshadow spans, and vertebral landing surroundings clear the full road, hull, and driving camera. Move, resize, or selectively edit existing fossils; retain coherent anatomy, grounded burial, and deliberate composition. Reconnect road supports and terrain after placement changes. Use the same final transforms for rendering and collision, rebaking variant colliders when the mesh itself changes. Retain distant scenery, sky, grazer, sandfall sources, lighting character, and deterministic placements unless directly affected; avoid rerandomizing the whole scene.
5. **Reconcile gameplay across the wider surface.** Keep clear glass and migrating sand meaningful, the optional dune skip optional, and both spine gaps physically playable. Reposition/resize devices, relevant hazard coverage, and local AI line targets as needed without adding new mechanics or changing shared handling. Check the unchanged camera first; inspect the complete variant before presenting it for review.
6. **Freeze, inspect, fix, and verify.** Supply the supporting agents with the same built revision and preview. Integrate concrete findings, then request only the bounded affected rechecks below. Deliver a complete playable variant, or explicitly identify the remaining failures if a budget is exhausted.
