## Working process

1. **Diagnose the current racing view.** Read the builder, maps, UVs, reflection/lighting setup, hazards and shared consumers. Inspect `?artPass=1&level=<slug>` from close oblique and chase views, including each distinct surface and lighting condition. Identify the visible limitation before baking assets or adding detail.
2. **Prove the intervention early.** Correct scale, construction, material channels or light response at the source. Use licensed maps, authored shaders or limited finish geometry where appropriate; there is no prescribed texture size, wear level or gloss. Halcyon's successful scan-based process is a reference, not a universal asphalt recipe.
3. **Carry it across the course.** Ordinary connectors and receiving areas need the same material quality as hero views. Keep metre scale through bends/banks and distinguish fine relief from construction. Review dry/wet, clear/opaque, safe/hazard and exposed/sheltered transitions at matching event phases. Reflection or emission must not imitate a hazard or hide its safe boundary.
4. **Check and finish.** Use matching in-engine views and short driving segments to assess seams, glare, shimmer and readability. Reassess a weak improvement rather than starting an indefinite critic loop. After acceptance, remove superseded maps/materials and temporary comparisons, retain editable sources/provenance/licences, refresh affected menu stills and close owned temporary probes. Do not start another level automatically.

## Boundaries and verification

Preserve route, width, bank, supported skin, gap/landing intervals, collision, hazard footprints, guidance, event timing, fleet handling and steering assistance 0.3. Cosmetic relief must not silently change the physical driving surface. Keep each world's [art direction](../../ART_BIBLE.md), borrowed-resource ownership and CPU/shape construction independent of texture loading. Shared exposure/lighting changes and unrelated scenery rebuilds require separate scope.

Measure cost on the same device/view/preset: frame intervals and spikes, draw calls/passes, triangles, texture memory and loading/compilation. Do not assume Halcyon's measured result provides spare budget elsewhere. High/Low must retain material identity and navigation cues; compression, mipmaps and bounded anisotropy are tools rather than substitutes for measuring shader/transparency cost. Automated checks do not establish foreground frame pacing or visual acceptance.

Follow the [level lifecycle](../../LEVEL_API.md), register changed resources in the [asset manifest](../../ASSET_MANIFEST.json), await new assets, support failure/retry and dispose only owned resources. Verify color/data color spaces and normal conventions. Run a production build and relevant [level checks](../../../tools/level-checks/README.md), including the maintained level smoke check. Refresh menu stills through the [preview workflow](../../../ARCHITECTURE.md#circuit-preview-refresh), including consumers of shared art.

This set retains the expansion campaign's **desktop-only** scope: no mobile, portrait, touch-emulation or mobile-landscape cases, including embedded cases in combined suites. Inspect runners before use. Keep autonomous checks hidden and bounded, without unsolicited videos or normal-speed full laps. Progress belongs in chat; remove owned temporary captures/reports rather than retaining evidence bundles. Handoff should state the actual improvement, checked behavior/cost, limitations and commit status.
