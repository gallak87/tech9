# Level and reward ideas

Parked ideas; priorities and implementation work belong in the [roadmap](../ROADMAP.md). Current mode and progression rules live in [architecture](../ARCHITECTURE.md#session-devices-and-replay) and the [level API](LEVEL_API.md).

- **Sidegrades:** prototype weapon or propulsion specializations with meaningful tradeoffs and complete icons, VFX and sound.
- **Magnetic Stabilizer:** revisit or replace it if its airborne steering assist cannot justify a distinct equipment role.
- **Carve:** prototype sustained slides and clean exits on a short banked section before authoring a course.
- **Escape:** prototype readable pursuit pressure, fair recovery and a supported destination in a suitable biome.
- **Assault:** revisit target-based combat after weapon expansion.
- **Ordinary-race aerial tricks:** prototype spins/flips while preserving driving and equipment controls.
