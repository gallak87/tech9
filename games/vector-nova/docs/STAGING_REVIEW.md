**Historical Gate 1 review — approved by user. Current scope and next gate are in ROADMAP.md.**

Run the built review at http://127.0.0.1:5180 (or development at http://127.0.0.1:5179). Both servers are kept running. The title screen introduces controls; Enter Proving Loop starts actual interactive practice, and Inspect Kestrel shows the same ship in neutral light.

Proposed direction:

- Ship: compact original split prow, pearl ceramic/graphite/titanium, canopy at centre and exposed twin rear drive units. Four hover shoes and restrained orange identity marks.
- Environment: elevated metropolitan utility viaduct, steel/concrete engineering, layered city scale and twin-crown landmark. First 432m is the representative track material/engineering study. Skyline massing and remaining course dressing are provisional; architecture detail and lighting still need production work.
- Camera: anticipatory chase, moderate 61–68° FOV, local-bank following and no shake. Alternative trackside camera and neutral inspection are available.
- Feel: immediate lateral steering, magnetic surface adhesion, airbrake slip, deliberate braking and energy-consuming boost. Barrier contact costs speed and reserve. Recover at checkpoint invalidates current lap. This model is surface-constrained; free flight and arbitrary-gravity loops are deferred.

Please approve these directions or request changes to the same staging build. No additional ships, circuits, AI, championship, pickups, production garage/menus or broad asset generation will begin without explicit approval.

## Verification limits

This is not a final release or a 9/10 claim. Formal critic gauntlets start after human approval. Physical controllers/mobile devices and subjective sound/handling quality are not certified. Browser emulation and headless Chrome screenshots/video are functional evidence, not proof of sustained 60fps on a representative foreground GPU workload. Initial synth audio and city massing remain provisional. No blind judges or formal production critics have run.

Automated keyboard playback is a verification controller in tools, not an in-game autopilot. Real input produces movement; the app is not a prerecorded or scripted racing demo.
