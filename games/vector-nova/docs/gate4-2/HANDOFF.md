Two deterministic, immediate drive-over effects. No inventory, activation button, item roulette, weapon, opponent attack or stored item. The user explicitly rejected collect-now/use-later mechanics for this pass; the original prompt's one-slot requirement is deferred for later consideration, not secretly required here.

These numbers are starting tuning values, not an acceptance score. Record final values and rationale. Use 4 speed strips and 2 shield pickups as an initial placement budget, revising only when short driving checks justify it. Introduce one forgiving strip early, then place later strips on readable exits/recovery straights. Offer modest lateral line choice within the existing road; do not force a novice into a blind corner, obstruct a braking zone or replace normal driving with continuous boosts. Shields should appear before demanding existing terrain with enough approach time to choose them. No route reauthoring or makeover of the whole city.

No stacking: a strip cannot compound itself, and a second shield does not add charges or extend an active shield. Retrigger eligibility is per device and forward lap traversal; standing, oscillating, resetting or driving backward must not farm an effect. A fresh lap/restart resets eligibility deliberately. Crossing a device while its same-type effect is active may give a subtle unavailable cue, never silently multiply power. Keep manual Shift behavior and energy risk/reward distinct; explicit manual boost may still spend reserve while the free strip is active.

## Simulation and lifecycle contract

Use track-local metres and lateral intervals, including banked/inverted surfaces. Device meshes conform to actual road triangles with safe depth offsets; no floating stickers, intersecting seals or light handoff flicker. Detect swept forward crossing across the old/new unwrapped distance and interpolated hull/lateral overlap at 120 Hz, including the lap seam; endpoint-only proximity fails at race speed. Require actual entry/exit/lap eligibility rather than a per-frame overlap event.

Keep effect state deterministic and serializable. Proposed tick order: expire existing timers; pass current modifiers into the vehicle step; resolve any shield consumption from collision; detect crossed devices and activate effects for the next fixed step; emit ordered events. Define exact behavior for same-tick expiry/impact/pickup and cover it in tests. Simulate zero effect time while paused or hidden. Audio/VFX cannot mutate physics.

Add opt-in modifiers to shared physics with unchanged defaults, scoped through the Halcyon adapter. Apply shield protection inside collision resolution, not by restoring speed/energy after arbitrary damage. The level 3 session imports the default magnetic step and owns its separate flight logic; do not alter that baseline or export global active pickup state.

The adapter owns reset/restore, pickup eligibility, presentation and cleanup. Fresh start/restart, scenario change and manual recovery clear temporary effects and stale visuals/audio; recovery does not re-arm already used devices for the same traversal. Replay records track, physics and feature/rules versions plus deterministic initial state. Both live and replay use the same core advance hook. Reject mismatches. Boosted practice records must not overwrite or compare against baseline/pickup-free records. Preserve future Time Trial as a separate fixed pickup-free ruleset; building a Time Trial menu is outside this gate.
