Instrumental cyberpunk progressive trance for futuristic racing, 128 BPM, F# minor. Deep, driving and hypnotic. Rolling sub-bass, punchy kick, crisp percussion, broad sustained synth chords and evolving filter sweeps. Minimal melody; movement comes from groove and texture, without bouncy lead riffs, busy arpeggios or piano. A two-minute arc: immediate groove, rising tension, huge bass-heavy EDM drop, spacious breakdown, bigger second drop, rhythmic outro. No vocals.
