# Skywell local art

Original editable Blender geometry for Skywell’s roadside arrival beacons, a matching decorative underside carriage and the compact Veil feeder lip. This is level-owned art; the canonical Zephyr kit is unchanged.

Skywell composes that kit with the existing Zephyr architecture, islands, silver water and sky. Its local deck finish supplies metric cassette seams and a satin coating; `precincts.js` composes close architectural shores and connected water streets around the approved route. `garden-finish.js` layers broad muted sage/olive courts over upward mineral tops while preserving the canonical triplanar grain, relief and pale cliff edges. Cultivated groves use Worldkit’s public Living Forest entry and canonical bark/leaf images, with roots seated on actual island triangles. Those borrowed assets retain their existing [Zephyr provenance](../zephyr/asset-manifest.json) and Worldkit provenance in `worldkit/modules/living-forest/assets/provenance.json`; this folder introduces no replacement shared kit.

The [selected Zephyr scene reference](../zephyr/zephyr-scene-reference.png) and its [exact source prompt](../zephyr/asset-manifest.json) establish weathered ivory masonry, formed bronze, dark recesses and sparse induction glass. These fixtures interpret that construction language without adding gates, hoops, point lights or driving surfaces.

Rebuild from the app directory:

```sh
/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender --background --python assets/levels/launchworks/build_arrival_art.py -- "$PWD"
```

`skywell-arrival-kit.blend` retains one named collection per asset and a non-exported wire reference of the original 44 m road keel for seating inspection. Each asset contains merged stone, bronze, recess and induction-glass material roles; the optional saddle has no emissive material. Exported meshes use metres, X lateral, Y up and +Z road-forward, with identity node transforms. Actual bounds, triangle counts and hashes live in [arrival-art-manifest.json](arrival-art-manifest.json).

Every receiver uses the same `arrival-beacon.glb`: a 70 m Gothic locator with a 10 m wide stone anchor shoe, formed bronze framing and recessed twin induction lancets. Its upper body is proportioned in the editable source before metric beveling, and runtime placement uses unit scale. Beacon centres sit 109 m from the road centre at Crown, 101 m at Bell Dive and 91 m at the other receivers, giving wide airborne entries another 60 m per side; the carriage’s stone bearing pads sit at ±28 m with a 4 m width. Four tapered bronze pickup webs connect the carriage’s U spine to two seated flanges under the unchanged keel. The flanges overlap its −3.2 m underside by 0.15 m, meet the webs with a 0.15 m overlap, and the webs seat 0.5 m into the spine. All pickup geometry remains below −3.05 m and within the carriage’s existing bounds. Runtime placement remains outside the road and actual flight/camera clearance still requires the production scenery test. `arrival-art.js` borrows its private GLB library into spatial instance batches, then disposes those instance buffers before the source geometry and materials. Furniture adds no driving surfaces; the current receiver scenery bake supplies its physical collision faces separately from the road.

`veil-spill-lip.glb` is a shallow closed limestone/bronze distributor with recessed front panels, fitted coping and four genuinely open source throats aligned to the existing water strands. It spans local X ±56 m, Y 0–10 m and Z −28–1 m; no stone crosses the four recorded mouth ranges at Y=0. A solid distributor bridges the central dry interval, and its right side receives the support arm at `[50, 6, -12]`. The optional `veilSource` input to `createSkywellArrivalArt()` loads this model into a static frame copied from the actual gameplay curtain’s world matrix, including its 0.85 X scale; its private geometry/materials retain the arrival owner’s lifetime. The model supplies only stone, bronze and recess roles; the original curtain geometry, material, opacity and water source remain unchanged.

`garden-terrace-grand.glb` supplies an original connected limestone promenade and pointed-arch balustrade for selected grand-island garden shores. Its four merged material roles contain real open bays, layered coping, generous canal and walking entrances, and six formed bronze anchor straps. The source imports the canonical `island-grand.glb` unchanged as a dimensional reference: Blender BVH rays measure its real mineral top and fit the straps to the eroded sides. The built promenade stays safely inside that top; no canonical mineral geometry is exported or altered. Counterbank’s canal mouth remains open between local angles −94° and −54°. The assembly shares the canonical island’s X/Z frame and Y=0 cultivated top, so runtime placement copies the selected island’s actual matrix, including nonuniform scale.

Rebuild this terrace separately from the app directory:

```sh
/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender --background --python assets/levels/launchworks/build_terrace_art.py -- "$PWD"
```

`skywell-garden-terrace.blend` retains the editable original assembly and a clearly named, unmodified canonical reference collection. Only the new limestone, pale coping, bronze and recess meshes are exported; preview lights and camera remain in the source file. Rebuilding also writes the preview to ignored `.experiments/skywell-garden-terrace-preview.png`, creating that directory when needed. The runtime GLB adds no images, lights or driving surfaces. Exact bounds, triangle count and hash live in [terrace-art-manifest.json](terrace-art-manifest.json).

`createSkywellTerraceArt({islands, stoneTexture, loadAsset})` in `terrace-art.js` loads that private GLB once for the Crown, Counterbank and Last Light main receiving shores. Its four-role clones sit in static frames copied from each selected island’s actual world matrix; all placement is complete before acquisition. Returned `blockers` keep planted roots outside the promenade. Disposal restores each selected clone’s previous decoration visibility and releases the helper’s private geometry/materials; the common limestone texture and reflection remain borrowed. The environment hides loose ornament roles on its private canonical island sources, leaving secondary shores as natural mineral landforms and preserving every rock body. `seatSkywellStrata()` reseats only unsupported pale wedge components on private source geometry; attached strata and canonical GLBs remain unchanged. The environment releases replaced originals once and retains the replacements through its normal source lifetime. Local `catapultBlurScale: 0.45` keeps peripheral racing blur restrained during the long flights; it adds no new pass and retains normal Low/reduced-motion behavior.

Gameplay, Sounds and Ship inspector share immediate road contact, Good/Great/Perfect rewards, confirmed-Miss feedback and completed-trick clicks through the common effects mixer. Jump Drive retains its dedicated landing sample.
