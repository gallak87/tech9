# Cindercoil original scene and geometry

The selected scene reference is [reference/cindercoil-scene-v1.png](reference/cindercoil-scene-v1.png), generated with the built-in `image_gen.imagegen` tool on 2026-09-29. The exact production prompt is preserved in [reference/prompt.txt](reference/prompt.txt). No uploaded image, external texture, borrowed mesh, or artist-specific style was used.

[build_cindercoil.py](build_cindercoil.py) translates the reference's layered basalt fault, three thick descending lava channels, ropy crust rafts, hollow columnar tower, and broken ivory/charcoal launch buttress into original Blender geometry. The editable source is `cindercoil-hero-kit.blend`; the only served export is `public/assets/levels/cindercoil/cindercoil-hero-kit.glb`. Rebuild with Blender 5.1.1 in background mode using the generator; it uses CPU modeling/export and makes no renders. `asset-manifest.json` records mesh and triangle counts.

The kit uses bulging closed cross-section lava tubes with fractured cooling shoulders, unequal layered rock faces, and sheared asymmetric crust plates with broken folds. Rock groups retain their proportions and root into the sculpted terrain. Runtime material detail adds domain-warped multi-scale color and normal variation without texture downloads; lake fissures use irregular cell boundaries. This is an authored lightweight approximation of the concept, not a photogrammetric reconstruction or fluid simulation. Runtime scenery adds stratified caldera terrain, instanced basalt and crust families, cold procedural ash clouds, a rough crusted lake shader, and small modeled vent tongues. The track owns the actual driving/launch surface; the asset's broken buttresses remain outside its flight envelope.

Wall opening, waterfall pulse, raft separation and vent state derive from the same deterministic level simulation time. No wall-clock animation, gameplay force or hidden collision is introduced here. Reduced motion removes ash drift and decorative lava oscillation while event phase remains accurate. High and low retain the same hero geometry, terrain and lighting; low reduces ash count. Blender sources and the generated reference are unserved working assets. Final browser, resource and critic evidence is recorded by the lane integrator, not certified by this provenance note.
