# Cindercoil — road finish

Use the [road workflow](README.md). Target `cindercoil`; preserve heat and spill readability.

## Starting point

The [production road](../../../src/levels/cindercoil/track/production.js) combines beveled construction with [procedural ceramic color, height and roughness maps](../../../src/levels/cindercoil/track/ceramic-textures.js). Pale, charcoal and oxide ceramics are mostly matte; Crown panels use a distinct darker glazed finish. The same construction can supply physical solids.

## Investigate

Check whether the surface conveys dense fired ceramic and formed plate edges at racing distance. Examine texture scale, bump response and the separation between matte bodies and glazed panels in both cool light and reflected lava warmth. Richer surface response may help, but glossy treatment across every tile would lose the existing material hierarchy. Choose the intervention from actual views, not a requirement for more cracks or soot.

## Keep and check

Compare an ordinary ceramic connector, the glazed Crown, the furnace roll and Curtain Reach; include the vent landing. Keep tile relief stable under moving highlights and ensure warm reflections retain detail. Preserve [spill openings and contact intervals](../../../src/levels/cindercoil/spill.js), warned surge boundaries, collision collection and supported road profiles. Run affected ceramic/road or spill checks alongside the shared runtime check. Shape and collision-only construction must remain independent of texture loading.
