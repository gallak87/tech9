How the comparison is aligned

Every candidate is copied from its source cell or documented crop. The three views share one scale, planned cell origins and one baseline. Their pixels are not warped, stretched, redrawn or automatically recentered pose by pose.

Transparent views use visible bounds with alpha greater than 32. Opaque sheets use documented silhouette bounds when supplied; their backgrounds remain untouched. Each sheet’s combined bounds determine its framing; the current sheet and candidate sheet are scaled uniformly to comparable silhouette heights. This makes costume differences easier to inspect, but does not prove identical in-game proportions. The dashed line marks the shared lower bound. On narrow screens the whole comparison scales down to fit.

The current reference follows HERO_IDLE_SHEETS and uses the game’s frame loader. The selectable transparency grid is CSS behind the canvas. Any checkerboard already painted into a generated sheet remains in its pixels and will still appear on every backdrop.
