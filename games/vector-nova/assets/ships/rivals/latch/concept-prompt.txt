Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: Original design reference sheet for a Blender-built rival hover racer in VECTOR//NOVA.
Primary request: Design LATCH, an ordinary production-spec opponent ship. It should look competent, practical, and a little less glamorous than a premium hero racer. An open-spine industrial racer: a narrow central rectangular tub with a rounded-off square single nose and a small forward angular cockpit, two exposed cylindrical engine nacelles running along the rear half on visibly separated straight cross spars. Short straight front canards are perpendicular to the body, neither crescent nor swept dagger wings. Negative-space channels visibly separate the center tub from the engines; the planform reads as a mechanical H with one nose extending forward. 6.5 metres long and 4.0 metres wide. Dusty blue-grey painted panels, charcoal ribs and restrained faded off-white maintenance bands.
Materials and construction: grounded futuristic engineered craft at real vehicle scale, subtle fine paint grain, brushed exposed metal, formed panels with real thickness, vents with depth, restrained fasteners, lightly worn edges. No toy plastic, oversize cartoon shapes, rust heap, weapons, skulls, spikes, wheels or landing gear. Low anti-gravity induction shoes under the hull. Restrained cyan-blue engine glow; no long exhaust plumes in this reference sheet.
Composition: clean neutral warm-grey studio sheet with a large front three-quarter view occupying the left two-thirds, consistent orthographic top view upper right and rear three-quarter lower right. All three views must show exactly the same geometry and livery. Full craft visible, comfortable margins, coherent construction usable for 3D modelling, clear silhouette and nozzle count.
Text: Only a small tasteful heading "LATCH / PRODUCTION RACER" and tiny "FRONT", "TOP", "REAR" view labels. Avoid hull numbers or decorative glyphs.
Style: mature industrial 3D game concept art, realistic material response, soft studio lighting, sharply readable shape, quieter finish than an ivory-and-vermilion premium racing ship.
