# Alder Signal

A short first-person field mission in the early-light forest. Three survey relays have lost their uplink. Follow the route, hold to restore each station, then return to the trailhead before the radio window closes. The feeling is focused, quiet urgency: grounded movement, readable equipment, restrained radio cues and a clear finish.

This is a complete small game used to test the new foundation. It deliberately leaves hunting, fishing and motorsports open for the user's next choice. The existing forest supplies atmosphere; authored relay props, mission logic, navigation, walking and sound supply the game.

The user delegated execution and creative scope. The [approved plan](./plan.json) records that authority, bounded deliverables, ownership, testing and the stopping point. No additional approval gate is needed for local implementation and testing.
