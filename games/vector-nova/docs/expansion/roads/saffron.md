# Saffron Leviathan — road finish

Use the [road workflow](README.md). Target `saffron`; coordinate with the separate [widening owner](../saffron.md) and [wide companion](saffron-wide.md).

## Starting point

The [road](../../../src/levels/saffron/track/road.js) uses opaque smoked/honey glass for the driving deck and physical amber glass at shoulders/underside. Procedural mineral veins and roughness supply internal variation. [Deposited sand](../../../src/levels/saffron/track/sand.js) drives both visible deposits and contact behavior; the environment has its own [baked weathering volume](../../../src/levels/saffron/environment/materials.js).

## Investigate

Study whether the road reads as thick fused mineral glass rather than uniformly colored plastic. Assess broad reflection, internal scale, edge thickness and the transition to granular sand under lavender daylight and skull shelter. More transmission is not automatically more convincing or affordable. Keep the opaque central deck's readability and preserve the distinction between glass and actual deposits.

## Keep and check

Compare an open connector, the skull descent, sand-strip entry/interior/exit and a vertebral receiving deck in both quality modes. Capture both sides of the glass/sand transition, including a sheltered deposit; sand must still read as a granular contact surface rather than another glossy glass panel. Check grazing reflections, transparency layering and repetitive veins through banks. Preserve supported profiles, baffles, gaps, sand drag and optional-skip rules. Asset changes shared with `saffron-wide` require checking both slugs and refreshing both menu stills; do not modify the widening task's geometry or approval state. The road's procedural glass and the environment's noise volume are separate systems—trace actual consumers before replacing either.
