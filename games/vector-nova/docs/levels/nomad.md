# Nomad Procession — the salt procession

An open 12.2 km downhill sprint with a supported 600 m runout, built in the existing Ravel worktree. Eleven colossal wheeled land carriers cross a white salt basin. The route winds through cargo halls, climbs exterior hulls, crosses roof decks and returns to lower holds, with ten mandatory unsupported transfers. The 50 m main road widens to 80 m at arrivals. Long supported stretches, moderate climbs and drops, and real gently upswept departure lips preserve racing speed between jumps. The Passing Window links a sustained exterior wall ride to the broad lower deck of an overtaking neighbor.
