# Helion Flare — road finish

Use the [road workflow](README.md). Target `helion`; preserve its thermal observatory identity.

## Starting point

The [road builder](../../../src/levels/helion/track/index.js) separates fresh, heat-cycled and sheltered cassettes, graphite structure, bevels and copper radiator edges. [Thermal finish shaders](../../../src/levels/helion/environment/materials.js) add world-space grain, relief, roughness and material-specific variation. The helper also affects architecture; [thermal surfaces](../../../src/levels/helion/environment/thermal-surfaces.js) are tied to local effects.

## Investigate

Check whether dense ceramic, dark graphite and metallic copper stay distinct under the enormous star and beneath cover. Identify whether material contrast, highlight shape, scale or relief is limiting the result. Improve the underlying finish while retaining believable differences between replacement and heat-cycled tiles. Do not use stronger emission or global exposure changes to manufacture a favorable comparison.

## Keep and check

Compare an exposed connector, a shaded braking shelf, the sunshield wall and the edge of a hot/cooling zone. Include active heated flooring and its transition to cooled/sheltered road, plus an optional jump's receiving area. Match the event phase in before/after views, checking ordinary and surge states where that floor changes. The finish must preserve the distinction between reflective ceramic and an active thermal hazard. Check for clipped highlights, lost shadow detail, shimmering grain and copper that overwhelms guidance. Keep thermal contact footprints, cooling, plasma visibility, sheltered main lanes and canopy clearances unchanged. Audit shared-helper consumers and run affected thermal/road checks; the surface pass must not retune hazards or prominence timing.
