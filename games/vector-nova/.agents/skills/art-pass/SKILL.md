# Art pass

Follow the [game rules](../../../AGENTS.md), [art direction](../../../docs/ART_BIBLE.md) and assigned brief. Choose the creative approach freely; preserve what the user enjoys.

- Before baseline review, locate the map's original imagegen concept images and prompt in source assets/provenance, including its unique feature. Read the prompt and visually inspect the actual images. Give these references to the critic and modeler, using them to assess composition, forms, materials, lighting and the map's identity. Later user-approved direction takes precedence.
- Judge overall environment quality and racing presentation, giving racing impact priority. Weight features by prominence, exposure and experiential importance: small, rarely visible defects matter less; brief signature moments can matter greatly. Evaluate moving elements across their encounter. Use free camera for diagnosis.
- Establish matching baseline views and a concrete visual target. Use an independent, brutal AAA art critic, calibrated to the intended style and references. Give it the racing views and keep its rubric stable; request one overall score out of ten, wins to retain and the one or two weaknesses whose improvement would most benefit the experience. The critic sets priorities; the artist chooses solutions. Score visible results.
- Address the dominant weakness with a coherent candidate, combining form, placement, lighting and materials where useful. Detail includes convincing construction, attachment and material transitions. Require a clear improvement at normal racing size before spreading a treatment.
- Use Blender for substantial modeling or sculpting. Rebuild, replace or rescale weak forms as needed within the brief. Preserve editable sources and assess the exported result in-game.
- Move into finish work when the main remaining weaknesses are finish-related. Honor explicit phase counts; otherwise direct remaining rounds toward the largest unresolved weakness. After two marginal rounds, reassess the diagnosis or change the authoring approach within the remaining budget.
- Keep trials cheap: prototype before expensive exports or bakes, and compare a few matched affected views. Review an ordinary stretch as well as a landmark. Broaden coverage at baseline, major milestones and completion; run checks appropriate to the change.
- Default to up to **16 improvement-and-review rounds**, stopping at **9/10 or higher** or the round cap, whichever comes first. Baseline review is separate. Honor user overrides and keep the best result. Finish verification, provenance and preview updates under the game rules. Store only each map's latest numeric score in [ART_SCORES.json](../../../docs/ART_SCORES.json); keep detailed findings in chat. Completed passes stay closed.
