# Kaida a1 visual limits

The current r2 body is provisional and slender. Fuller proportions, fine finger/thumb contact and more natural weight transfer remain optional refinement. No regeneration or extra motion download is required for this alpha.

Magenta hair, cyan jacket, dark trousers and boots, and the separate magenta blade establish the current design. Small brown wristbands remain in the supplied geometry despite exposed wrists in the reference. Finger webs and the thumb contour need a later close-up art pass.

A replacement body requires a new model identity and verification of skinning, clip compatibility, dimensions and sword grip. Preserve the current release in Git before making that change. Evaluate actual gameplay motion; structural import success does not certify appearance.
