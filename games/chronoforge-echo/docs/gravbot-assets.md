# Obsidian Gravbot redesign

Archive note (September 17): superseded Vex/Gravbot sources and rejected attempts now live under [experiments/2026-09-ui-and-art](../experiments/2026-09-ui-and-art/README.md). Historical source paths below can be resolved through its [archive manifest](../experiments/2026-09-ui-and-art/archive-manifest.json). Current selected assets remain in `public/assets/`.

The active Gravbot is now a dark armored humanoid bot based on the original Chronoforge identity in `../chronoforge/src/assets/gravbot_idle.png`, also shown in the user's reference. Broad shoulder plates, a helmet/visor, separated mechanical limbs and a round teal forearm gravity weapon distinguish it from the retired stone counterweight. Stats, skills, encounters and animation timing are unchanged.

The selected immutable source is `public/assets/gravbot/obsidian-gravbot-source.png`: 1536×1024 RGBA, 1,961,025 compressed bytes, SHA-256 `048d1288122d99fa6050e9878fbcde779a2913f793f542bf0c6c13fb06c2fe8f`. It has 1,021,220 fully transparent pixels. Its generated alpha, pale metal edges and teal weapon cores are preserved without color keying or alpha rewriting. The retired stone source and two rejected checker-backed generation attempts remain unchanged in the repository; neither rejected attempt is in the asset manifest.

All three exact built-in `image_gen.imagegen` prompts, reference, original output paths and selected/rejected status are recorded in `docs/gravbot-art-prompts.json`. Source alpha measurements are in `evidence/gravbot-refresh/source-alpha.json`. No CLI image generation or procedural replacement was used.

`GRAVBOT_ART` in `src/enemy-frames.js` selects the source through the existing manifest. Six measured crops use one fixed native source scale of 0.20, preserving anatomical scale; idle stands about 86 native pixels tall. Foot anchors are measured per pose. There is no new importer or runtime key mask.

`node tests/gravbot-art.mjs` passed with no page/console/asset errors. Its explicitly documented party/camera fixture renders the actual Emberline gate sentry, then lets production `Game.update` schedule the guard encounter and Gravbot's attack. Evidence includes `world-emberline-guard.png`, `battle-idle.png` and six timed `enemy-action-*.png` captures under `evidence/gravbot-refresh/`. This is visual fixture evidence, not a claim of earned campaign progression. The import contact sheet `six-poses-dark-pale.png` separately verifies all six poses at world scale 0.85 and battle scale 1.45 on dark and pale backgrounds. Visual review confirmed clear robot anatomy, complete weapon/feet, clean limb gaps, stable ground contact and correct forearm contact with the defender. The temporary browser closed; the shared Vite server was left running.
