# Nightweir — Blackstart

The [selected stormwater city concept](../../assets/levels/nightweir/reference/nightweir-v1.png) establishes an inhabited hydroelectric gorge descending toward a working harbor. Worldspire remains a separate level.

The race descends 3.88 km over 13.58 km of municipal roads, followed by a 650 m supported braking quay: 14.23 km total. Eight physical jumps and three corkscrews interrupt sustained banked reversals. Great Spillway and Harbor Leap cross 440 m horizontal gaps with approximately 4.8 seconds of flight; Turbine Leap crosses 300 m in approximately 3.4 seconds. Long downhill receivers accommodate the fleet at cruise and boosted entry speeds. Shared steering and gravity are unchanged. The level-owned approach camera pulls back gently while preserving forward city views.

The city should read as connected occupied neighborhoods and working civil infrastructure. Human-scale windows, public streets, masonry returns, galleries, rooted foundations and distinct rooflines establish scale around the monumental impoundments. Editable Blender geometry and matching physical solids are authored against the production road and exported ballistic clearance volumes. The runtime city is a losslessly gzip-packed GLB; model provenance records its unpacked hash. Named collision ranges map triangle contacts back to authored structures. The Canal bend has a bank-keyed pressure-exchange hall with a tall open machinery bay, a lower occupied service wing and two pressure trunks terminating in collector casings. Lower Works uses unequal roofed wings around an open operating court, exposing its pressure header, crane and occupied galleries from the road. Hillside public streets follow local terrain levels; lower rooms and galleries occupy the visible bases of the quarter and Market blocks. The Market freight intake is a deep opening within those floors. These major working assemblies are separate deterministic Blender helpers, with common materials and route clearance volumes.

Municipal asphalt reuses Halcyon’s licensed CC0 scan at its 2.05 m repeat, with a rough neutral dry finish, sparse puddles and wetter runoff beside the gutters. Concrete uses Helion’s licensed 4.4 × 2.2 m substrate with formwork, rain and basin-aware waterline staining. Selected fired-clay buildings use the project-generated masonry base color at a 1.25 m repeat with restrained derivative relief. Pitched industrial roofs use generated weathered zinc at an 8 m repeat with authored seams, clerestories and drainage. Connected geological banks use a project-generated fractured strata texture at a 40 m repeat; triplanar gorge shading borrows Shatterline’s project-generated limestone for fine detail. Gentler slopes blend a separate generated damp scree and moss surface at a 6 m repeat, using the authored terrain normals rather than visible triangle boundaries. Interior parallax, recessed room glazing with grouped, restrained illumination and shielded fixtures complement physical construction; short-range vertex contact shading preserves construction joints. A separate broad sky-visibility bake is stored in vertex alpha and attenuates only indirect illumination; moving sun and working lights remain live. Neither bake encodes sun direction.

Blackstart restores power ahead of the driver: occupied rooms and service lights illuminate across the next district while heavy turbine rotors spool up. A working landmark retains one of the fixed service-light slots during each hero flight; authored distance ranges reserve the same pool for visible machinery halls and public galleries on ordinary bends. Point-light counts stay constant throughout the race so district changes do not compile new lighting variants. Retained pressure-control gates lift on approach. The old on-road water curtains and their invisible grip/drag changes are retired. Narrow source-fed discharges and large impoundment cascades remain outside the racing corridor. Water reuses Nacre’s presentation clock, analytic basin waves and Tech9-derived mipmapped ripple normals. Flow runs during title/countdown, continues into racing and freezes on pause or reduced motion; gates remain distance-driven. Water and turbine materials borrow the renderer-owned reflection probe. Local owners dispose their geometry, materials and ripple/rock textures without disposing that borrowed probe.

The turbine hydraulic kit exports connected feed pipes, discharge mouths, receiving pools and downstream tailraces alongside the city. Runtime water follows those authored anchors: volumetric jets enter contained pools, aerated impact water carries foam downstream, and GPU-animated droplets and thinning mist receive the existing work lights. All effects share the presentation water clock and the fixed light pool; they add no driving collision or on-road grip changes. Pool surfaces and jets own their geometry and materials while borrowing the ripple texture and reflection probe from their existing owners.
