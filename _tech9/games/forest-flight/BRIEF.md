# Forest Flight

A compact forest airfield flying game with a forgiving high-wing trainer and a quicker low-wing sport airplane. Select either aircraft, taxi and take off, explore the valley through three optional scenic markers, then return for a landing. Free flight and immediate retry keep the focus on flying.

The forest airstrip has graded clear approaches, runway markings, apron, hangar, windsock and useful landmarks. A broad bounded landscape gives the aircraft room to turn. Each aircraft has distinct handling and silhouette, animated propeller, wheels and control surfaces. Desktop keyboard controls, chase/cockpit cameras, readable flight instruments, stall feedback, pause, recovery, mute and graphics selection complete the experience.
