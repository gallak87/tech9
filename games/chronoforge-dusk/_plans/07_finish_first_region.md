# 07 — Finish the first region

**Type:** several focused implementation and playtest assignments. **Status:** future outline.

**Requires:** 06's complete loop and the owner's accepted first-region design.

## Outcome

One polished region with a beginning, development, climax, and payoff that makes the owner want to keep playing.

Deepen the working game in place:

- Complete Vex and Rune through the proven character workflow, preserving their original identities and distinct personalities. Evaluate them with Kaida in real traversal and battle.
- Add meaningful discoveries and a few distinct encounters selected by the region design. Give the region a satisfying final confrontation and local resolution.
- Finish the intended crew combat: useful techniques/support/status effects, positional area targeting, battle items, and coordinated dual-techs for each pair plus Aeon Sunder when the accepted progression calls for it.
- Develop small, meaningful skill branches and weapon/armor/accessory choices. Make rewards matter before the region ends.
- Deepen the local food/ore/energy/renown economy and selected settlement upgrades enough to support the story and exploration. Leave later civilization tiers for later content.
- Complete coherent Map, Party, Inventory, Skills, Quests, Save, Settings, and settlement access as relevant to the actual region. Remove feature stubs.
- Refine crew dialogue, NPC interactions, and environmental storytelling with the owner. Preserve the agreed tone; do not add whimsical lore merely to fill space.
- Finish environment cohesion, audio, effects, transitions, readability, and performance. Recheck the native game after final presentation changes.

Use [VALIDATION.md](../VALIDATION.md) and the accepted design to form bounded tasks when this milestone is reached. Asset and game work may proceed in parallel, with one integration owner and isolated comparisons.

## Done

The owner plays the complete region using actual traversal and combat, including ordinary rewards and progression, defeat/retry, save/resume, and the ending. The accepted assets work together, the core systems have practical use, and material defects or limitations are recorded honestly.

Do not begin producing every remaining region while this one is still thin. Choose the next addition through 08 after reviewing what this region taught us.
