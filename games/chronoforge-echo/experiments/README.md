# Experiments and design history

These files preserve useful design work and rejected or superseded assets. They are outside `public/` and are not imported by the game or copied into its production build.

- [September 2026 UI and art archive](2026-09-ui-and-art/README.md): interactive menu/ATB concepts, earlier Vex and Gravbot designs, failed transparency attempts, prompts and original-path mapping.

The accepted implementation remains in `src/` and `public/`; executable regression checks remain in `tests/`, and their captures remain in `evidence/`.
