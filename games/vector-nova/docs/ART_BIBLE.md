# Art direction

## Shared principles

The whole world and the raceway must share an identity: surface, edge silhouette, underside/supports, ordinary connectors, foreground, middle distance, sky and light. A strong hero vista cannot compensate for unfinished stretches.

Engage early and spread climbs, drops, banks, corkscrews and inversions through the lap, with readable recovery space. Do not stretch flat straights to meet a length target or cluster every dramatic feature in one short sequence.

Readability order: upcoming road/corner, craft/propulsion, gameplay feedback, decoration. Use real geometry, meaningful large/medium/small forms, coherent material response and grounded construction. Avoid bloom hiding form, screenshots posing as scenery, palette-swap levels and detail that vanishes at driving speed.

High/low presets retain the same world and navigation cues. Reduced motion restrains flashes and environmental movement. Use generated imagery only for appropriate asset/material roles, with exact provenance; preserve editable sources and licences.

## Kestrel

Approved angular futuristic jet silhouette: slender split nose, swept planform, layered ceramic/graphite/titanium construction, dark canopy and restrained vermilion markings. Blue exhaust should feel powerful in cruise and grow under boost without hiding the hull. Use the actual Blender model in gameplay and inspection; the concept image is reference only.

## Existing worlds

- **Halcyon Viaduct:** elevated metropolitan infrastructure at blue hour, warm low horizon and cool steel/sky. Dark composite deck, pale concrete spine, engineered barriers and amber guidance. Layered authored neighborhoods, supports meeting the city floor, canal courts and split-crown landmark; preserve the varied introductory route.
- **Asterion Orbital:** sunlit orbital shipyard above a living planet, with ivory alloy, graphite and planetary bounce. Smooth induction cassette panels, real open lattice spans, broad cyan/magenta/blue road sections and tall open U frames. Designed dockyards and service structures, large lit moon and structured stellar sky.
- **Nacre Tempest:** ocean storm, basalt spires, luminous reefs and tide observatory. Pearl/ceramic scales, translucent pressure inserts, swept fin edges and distinct surface/submerged districts. Actual water depth and motion, readable jumps/landings, restrained lightning and a monumental creature; retain the human-approved track/water direction and final lane feedback.

## Vesper Canopy

Colossal enclosed living trees, connected oversized foliage and an amber resin road with restrained transmission and a thin glowing orange curb. Keep the giant hollow complete around genuine route portals and preserve dark-to-lit contrast against a cloudy forest sky. Broad leaves need curved silhouettes and vein detail at fly-camera distance as well as canopy mass from the road. The retired creature remains source-only; raised petals, roadside bulbs/stalks and jump-edge beacons stay removed. Asset construction details remain in [Vesper provenance](../assets/levels/vesper/provenance.json); [Vesper learnings](expansion/vesper/learnings.md) capture reusable workflow steers.

## UI and feedback

Warm off-white/graphite, technical numerals, vermilion active accents, generous spacing and real controls. Keep telemetry and notices away from the craft, upcoming road and touch controls. Moderate chase FOV, no default camera shake, and road-local camera clearance.

Halcyon's immediate devices use manufactured amber-white Vector strips and symbol-marked teal Aegis hardware. Show collection, active effect, hit and expiry with form/timing as well as color. Ship feedback stays subordinate to the silhouette.

The [active expansion briefs](EXPANSION_HANDOFF.md) own the seven new worlds' specific directions. Do not apply Halcyon asphalt or Asterion U frames as a universal template.
