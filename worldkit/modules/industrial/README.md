# Industrial assemblies

`createIndustrialKit({ finish })` from `@worldkit/environment/industrial` provides original metre-scale press frames, factory bays, roller banks, drive wheels, ejector housings, channel cassettes, cooling banks, gantries, service towers, motor housings, feed rolls, die jaws, bearing pedestals, drive gearboxes, hydraulic supply banks, service decks, open transfer beds, louver panels and guided counterweight assemblies. Colossus Works is the first consumer. Assemblies share bevelled geometry and enamel/metal materials. Working assemblies include connected pipe feeds, removable access panels, machined shafts, fabricated webs and flanges. Rough dark cast bearing bodies, clean coated removable covers and turned working surfaces have separate material responses. Enamel keeps its broad reflection shape with restrained substrate noise; working sheets use quieter grain so the consuming level can author process wear at construction scale. Counterweight guides and weights are separate assemblies; the consumer owns weight motion and tensioned rope endpoints. Keep their working faces visible from the consuming game’s driving camera; service hardware hidden behind continuous walls adds cost without improving the scene.

The kit returns `prototypes`, `materials`, `createAssembly(name)` and idempotent `dispose()`. Placed assemblies borrow its geometry/materials; remove them before disposing the kit. The caller owns placement, lights, collision and moving parts. Geometry construction needs no browser.

Optional `createIndustrialFinish(renderer)` owns original generated steel and enamel textures and exposes `ready`, `ownedTextures`, `bind(material, kind, coordinates)` and idempotent `dispose()`. Material kinds are `steel`, `shell`, `dark`, `cast` and `machined`; default world coordinates project at physical scale, including instanced and batched geometry. Machined kit parts retain an `industrialCoord` attribute before assembly transforms; the `part` projection follows their original lathe axis and fades fine tool marks with pixel derivatives. The `road` projection consumes a caller-provided `steelCoord` varying in metres. A kit borrows its supplied finish; dispose the finish after its material borrowers. Without a renderer no image is loaded, supporting CPU geometry checks. `industrialSteelURL` is the canonical Vite-resolved asset URL. Exact generation prompt and hash are in [steel provenance](assets/provenance.json) and [enamel provenance](assets/enamel-provenance.json).
