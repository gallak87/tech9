# Industrial assemblies

`createIndustrialKit({ finish })` from `@worldkit/environment/industrial` provides original metre-scale press frames, factory bays, roller banks, drive wheels, ejector housings, channel cassettes, cooling banks, gantries, service towers and motor housings. Colossus Works is the first consumer. Assemblies share bevelled geometry and enamel/metal materials.

The kit returns `prototypes`, `materials`, `createAssembly(name)` and idempotent `dispose()`. Placed assemblies borrow its geometry/materials; remove them before disposing the kit. The caller owns placement, lights, collision and moving parts. Geometry construction needs no browser.

Optional `createIndustrialFinish(renderer)` owns original generated steel and enamel textures and exposes `ready`, `ownedTextures`, `bind(material, kind, coordinates)` and idempotent `dispose()`. Material kinds are `steel`, `shell` and `dark`; default world coordinates project at physical scale, including instanced and batched geometry. The `road` projection consumes a caller-provided `steelCoord` varying in metres. A kit borrows its supplied finish; dispose the finish after its material borrowers. Without a renderer no image is loaded, supporting CPU geometry checks. `industrialSteelURL` is the canonical Vite-resolved asset URL. Exact generation prompt and hash are in [steel provenance](assets/provenance.json) and [enamel provenance](assets/enamel-provenance.json).
