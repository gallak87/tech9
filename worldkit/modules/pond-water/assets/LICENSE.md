# Shore rock textures

`shore-rock-{diff,nor_gl,rough}.jpg` are unmodified 1K maps from [Rock Boulder Cracked](https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_boulder_cracked) by Dimitrios Savva (photography) and Dario Barresi (processing), licensed [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

Original filenames: `rock_boulder_cracked_{diff,nor_gl,rough}_1k.jpg`. These bundled images are the editable source maps. Reeds, cattails, lily pads, flowers and rock geometry are generated by `src/PondShore.js`.
