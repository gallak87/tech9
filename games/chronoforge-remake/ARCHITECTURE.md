# Chronoforge · The Unwritten Hour

## Reference and scope
The sole original-game reference is `../chronoforge/src/`. Preserve Kaida (magenta-haired sword warrior, cyan jacket), Vex (violet hood, cyan void magic), Rune (goggled, gold-armored sentinel), the resource ribbon and bottom exploration hints, the inset seven-tab overlay (Map, Party, Inventory, Skills, Quests, Save, Settings), Q/E or left/right and 1–7 tab navigation, and ATB formation: heroes left, enemies right, three status rows below, commands lower right. Original gaps include no relationship narrative or ending, disconnected one-tile travel, absent town commerce, and incomplete skill effects. No original code or visual assets are imported.

## Runtime
Native ES modules, Canvas 2D pixel world at 960×600, semantic HTML overlays at the same aspect ratio, no production dependencies. `game.js` owns the finite states title/world/dialogue/battle/reward/ending and input. `world.js` owns a contiguous world, collision, doors and local pathfinding. `render.js` composes cached pixel environments, sorted actors, particles and frame-based sprites. `ui.js` renders menus and HUD. `battle.js` owns ATB, targeting, timed actions and rewards. `data.js` holds authored content. `state.js` owns progression, equipment, economy and saves. `audio.js` synthesizes an original adaptive score and effects.

## Shared state and persistence
One serializable versioned state stores position/interior return point, heroes, item instances with unique IDs and upgrade levels, money/ore/food/renown, learned skills, narrative flags, discovered regions, opened chests, cleared encounters, side quests, choices and elapsed time. Derived stats have one calculation path. Save after completed events and explicit manual saves; transient battle and dialogue objects never enter saves. Defeat retries restore a pre-battle snapshot; retreat restores the snapshot at the last town. Three manual slots plus an independent autosave. Validate before loading and report storage errors.

## World and narrative
Eight physically connected regions: Haventide → Emberline → Orbital Reach → Last Crown; Forest Veil and Mire Bog south; Crater Ember and Frost Canyon north. Wide edge corridors render both neighbors without a fade. Four town centers contain actual door-linked rooms for rest, trade, smithing and narrative discovery. Click-to-walk uses collision-aware paths; WASD/arrows and Shift provide direct walk/run. Visible encounters, chests, story landmarks and side quests make exploration useful.

Kaida searches for her sister Iona, lost during the collapse. Vex helped design the machine that caused it; Rune was its rescue sentinel and disobeyed an order to save them both. They met aboard Iona's evacuation ferry. Three anchor memories reveal Iona survived as the Chronoforge's living reference clock. Restoring anchors unlocks pair techniques. A final collective technique severs the Architect's closed loop, returning Iona and giving the world an uncertain, shared future. Optional rescues change the homecoming.

## Progression and battle
Team XP produces individual stat growth and skill points; skill prerequisites and levels gate advancement. Weapon, armor and accessory item instances have hero compatibility, required levels and three upgrade ranks. Deterministic first-clear loot, lower repeat rewards, chests, shops and sell/forge prices create a finite balanced campaign with repeatable recovery options. Rest is always available free in Haventide.

ATB fills independently by speed, pauses during command selection in Wait mode, and resolves actions through windup/strike/recovery with a single impact event. Attack, Tech, Defend, Item, and Link commands support enemy/ally/all targets. Waiting for allies deliberately advances time so pair/triple readiness is attainable. Links atomically consume every participant's full ATB and MP, reject downed/unready members, and unlock from story bonds. Guard, shields, healing/revival, drain, slow, taunt and immunity affect actual combat. The final boss has telegraphed phases.

## Settlement (explicitly in scope)
Haventide has a walkable commons with seven buildable plots and a town hall. Farms produce food, mines ore, extractors energy; the forge reduces equipment-upgrade costs, barracks grant party HP, the archive grants magic, and walls grant defense. Buildings have three visible ranks with town-hall prerequisites. The hall improves on restored-anchor milestones. Production advances during active world play and battles, is banked with capacity limits, and is collected at the hall; no hidden offline clock. Building/upgrading consumes coins, ore and energy atomically. Saved construction state, worker dialogue, production readouts and a homecoming consequence complete the loop. Initial materials permit meaningful construction immediately; all combat remains finishable without compulsory grinding.

## New asset workflow
Imagegen creates original transparent frame atlases from textual identity descriptions only; no reference pixels or original generation code are supplied. Saved prompts and sheet coordinates accompany assets. The three hero atlases provide directional walking, running, idle, attacks, casting and hurt. `assets/sprites/manifest.json` maps every enemy, equipment item, supply, non-settlement NPC and salvage-cache state to a generated sheet cell. Enemy sheets have six frames per row for idle, walk, attack and hurt/collapse; the Architect has three visual phase rows. NPC rows contain six idle/gesture frames. The renderer measures alpha bounds at load, keeps each actor's standing scale through its other poses, anchors feet, and fails on missing or blank mapped frames. No procedural enemy/item fallback remains. Pixel terrain, architecture, effects and settlement art retain the existing code rendering. No static-character rotation substitutes for animation. Inspect sheets and browser screenshots at runtime scale.
