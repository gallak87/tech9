`build_saffron.py` is the reproducible editable Blender source; `leviathan-kit.blend` is its saved Blender 5.1.1 scene. Run Blender in background with the script to export `public/assets/levels/saffron/leviathan-kit.glb`. Modeling and export used CPU only. The kit includes a road-clear skull vault with integrated rostral, orbital, nasal and occipital cortex, real thick orbital/skylight apertures, eight seeded cortical pores, two long fracture openings and selective longitudinal erosion channels. Three vertebral variants carry joined cortical processes, raked neural laminae and concave centrum endplates; three twisted rib-blade variants have enlarged roots and closed fractures with recessed cancellous interiors. Two displaced dune families, two stratified shelves and a bone-shelf sandfall with six irregular sheets spread through depth complete the landscape kit. The colossal low dune grazer has a continuous tapered shoulder/body, nine broad swept overlapping shields with thick recessed margins, a convex shovel cap, a sheltered underside mineral-filter basket and small protected sensory slits. Matte dark umber, irregular dusty ivory corner wear, warm cavities and ochre muscle replace the former glossy segmented animal. Merged material families bound draw calls; the grazer uses four meshes. Broad mineral wear and cranial shelter are baked vertex colors, complemented by the level's filtered weathering. The anatomy and generator remain editable. Landscape shading, sand boundary motion and creature animation are authored in the level.

Approximation: surface porosity is represented by real large openings, mineral seams and layered anatomy rather than dense sculpted microcavities. The grazer uses a fixed world transect, surveyed ground heights and one continuous shader deformation, rather than a biological simulation. +Z is modeled/runtime forward; the shovel leads and the tail extends -Z. A forward crossing 180 m before Leviathan Vista starts one epoch per lap. The head rises during seconds 2–4, grazes through second 10 and dives through second 14, with body lag and tail burial. The transect is authored farther ahead on the left dune field so the initial breach enters the chase view at racing speeds. Pause, inspection, snapshot restore and replay reuse the same epoch; manual recovery retains consumed lap eligibility. Most length stays below the terrain. One bounded ridge/furrow mesh and 320 points (120 at low quality) express sand displacement; reduced motion suppresses grains and lateral sway. Sandfall surfaces and grains complement fixed geological meshes.

`bake_weathering.mjs` generates the project-authored periodic noise lookup used by bone, sand and armor. Run `node assets/levels/saffron/bake_weathering.mjs` to reproduce `public/assets/levels/saffron/weathering-noise-v1.r8`: a 64³ single-channel unsigned-byte volume (262,144 bytes), uploaded with linear filtering and mipmaps. It replaces repeated shader hash calculations; world-space material scales, geological bands and derivative relief remain authored in the level. No external image or texture is used.

Module origins, exported bounds, polygon counts and source/runtime SHA-256 hashes are in `asset-manifest.json`. All authored files are project-owned; no third-party attribution or license dependency was introduced.

The selected lavender panorama is exported unchanged to `public/assets/levels/saffron/saffron-sky-v2.png`; its prompt and sources remain in [sky provenance](SKY_PROVENANCE.md). Sky projection overlaps the longitude seam and blends the poles, with a single source moon and separately authored scene lighting.

The original sand/grain shaders use the project weather module and forest adapter as architectural references for bounded world-space layers, shelter and coordinated exposure. No source code, textures or bootstrap from those references is copied. Road-relative skull cover replaces their static X/Z surface field.
