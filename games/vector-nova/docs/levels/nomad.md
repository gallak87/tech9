# Nomad Procession — the salt procession

An open 15.0 km downhill sprint with about 2.9 km of physical descent and a supported 600 m runout. Eleven colossal wheeled land carriers cross five progressively lower salt shelves toward a white basin. Winding cargo floors, exterior climbs, roof decks and lower holds connect ten mandatory unsupported transfers. Four large launches cross substantial open gaps into the broad roofs of High Balcony, Portside Dive, Passing Deck and Terminus. The Passing Window follows a sustained exterior wall ride; Saltwind preserves its service hall before exiting the starboard portal into an outboard final launch. The 50 m main road widens to 80 m at ordinary arrivals and 100–120 m on the long roof catches, accommodating real cruise and boost trajectories. The four main transfers span roughly 528–742 m; boost-facing launch straights and long receiving roofs leave useful braking room after powered flights. Portside provides 1,029 m before its first strong turn; Passing has 1,824 m of roof. Coasting uses a different grounded interception line. [Suspended approach lights](../../src/levels/nomad/air-guidance.js) trace the open aerial corridor to the correct moving receiver and continue down its landing zone.

## Moving road and collision

The [route model](../../src/levels/nomad/track/model.js) owns nominal geometry, actual road coverage, carrier transforms and transfer windows. The [motion adapter](../../src/levels/nomad/motion.js) exposes `frameAtTime(distance, time)`, `surfaceAtTime(distance, lateral, time)` and `motionAt(distance, time)`. Nominal sampling remains available for asset placement and collision construction; rendering uses the player's interpolated `presentationTime`.

The world uses the convoy's uniformly translating reference frame. Salt travel and wheel rotation express the shared 416.7 m/s forward ground speed (1,500 km/h); each carrier also has real bounded lateral and longitudinal translation. Ordinary carriers use 16 m longitudinal and 5 m lateral amplitudes with 36–44 second periods. The overtaking pair uses opposing 28 m longitudinal amplitudes, 6 m lateral amplitude and a 38 second period. Vertical displacement follows the 18% longitudinal salt grade. The exact [ground profile](../../src/levels/nomad/ground-profile.js) adds broad lateral shelves, exposing genuinely lower carriers across the large transfers; `groundHeightAt(x,z)` and `groundNormalAt(x,z)` describe their connected physical skin. These same pure equations drive visible hulls, roads, pickups, navigation paint and physics. The [ground motion](../../src/levels/nomad/ground-motion.js) moves fixed landmarks backward along the longitudinal grade in both rendering and the native `nomad-ground` collision group.

The [collision world](../../src/levels/nomad/collision.js) combines the exact running skins, guards, retained Blender hulls, end bogies, route load frames, receiving crown, suspended freight, service installations and salt plane. The added Blender assemblies use the same evaluated faces for rendering and native collision. Saltwind alone substitutes its locally opened hull and matching major hull collider; all other carriers retain the shared body. Carrier triangles retain named motion groups. [Dynamics](../../src/levels/nomad/dynamics.js) stages their continuous rigid translations in native Rapier; the authored moving surface chart remains authoritative for grounded support. Launches inherit material velocity and arrivals use relative velocity. Each [racer runtime](../../src/levels/nomad/runtime.js) owns its simulation clock, with snapshot/restore and reset preserving carrier phase. The [interception pilot](../../src/levels/nomad/pilot.js) steers through ordinary inputs toward the predicted moving arrival. Its grounded interception plan and airborne policy share the intended propulsion: hard rivals use ordinary boost on the major transfers, while easy and medium rivals use the coasting line. Normal reserve thresholds still apply; overshoots remain physical misses. It supplies no hidden launch impulse or midair correction.

The convoy also moves while the start lights are up. The locked grid plays a negative-time pre-roll into phase zero, staging the actual Native skins, whole-hull ship poses, pickups, wheels, ground and dust together. Green returns the exact deterministic initial snapshot before ordinary physics begins; race timing and reserve stay untouched during the lights. Pausing, inspection and suspension hold that phase.

The four major transfers use [level-owned recovery](../../src/levels/nomad/recovery.js). Valid touchdowns immediately bank their receiving carrier. Late misses, after crossing 65% of the horizontal gap, recover 120 m onto that carrier's broad straight at 100 m/s; early failures return 220 m before the lip with the same manageable speed. Automatic recovery retains its 1.5-second delay. Manual recovery follows the same destination policy while retaining the shared invalid-attempt penalty and validated jump checkpoint credit. Recovery stores carrier/jump identities in snapshots and resolves their current moving road position when used.

Mint deck markings identify a 50 m [magnetic launch-assistance zone](../../src/levels/nomad/launch-assist.js) before each major lip. The player's grounded controller gently aligns heading and sideways velocity toward the forecast moving-deck interception and supplies bounded forward acceleration when a weak launch would fall short of the safe receiving inset. Braking, airbraking, strong steering, an unsupported hull or the takeoff lip disables the field. It supplies no airborne correction. Rival motion explicitly retains its existing unassisted interception policy; the player's assist configuration participates in replay identity.
