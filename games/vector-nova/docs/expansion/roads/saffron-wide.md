# Saffron Wide — road finish companion

Use the [road workflow](README.md) and [Saffron material brief](saffron.md). Target `saffron-wide`; this does not authorize further widening or promote the experimental variant.

## Starting point

The variant has its own [40 m route](../../../src/levels/saffron-wide/track/model.js), [glass road](../../../src/levels/saffron-wide/track/road.js), solid profiles and deposit implementation. It reuses original Saffron assets. Check the current divergence and coordinate with the [active widening brief](../saffron.md) before editing.

## Investigate

Pursue the same material quality as the original, while judging the wider surface on its own. Check physical feature size across the width, apparent glass thickness, reflection breakup and the scale of internal veins and sand. Reuse a successful finish where appropriate; avoid stretching a whole-width pattern or blindly copying the original's lateral coordinates.

## Keep and check

Verify an open wide deck, the cranial bank, a sand boundary and both receiving decks at `?artPass=1&level=saffron-wide`. Keep the 40 m supported width, distinct identity, adapted baffles, collisions and deposited-sand behavior unchanged. Check original Saffron whenever shared assets change. Refresh the variant's menu still as well as any affected original; report this finish work separately from the widening owner's phase gates.
