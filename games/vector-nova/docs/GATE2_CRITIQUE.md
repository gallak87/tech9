## Review method

After the integrator declares a round ready, launch the actual local build, drive real keyboard controls and capture independent close ship, normal chase, bank, tunnel and distant views. Walk the full course in motion, including corner entry/exit, brake/airbrake, boost, impact/recovery, pause and restart. Compare visible control direction against camera projection and observe both slope-rest and bank-rest over time. Record actual scenario and simulation time with each finding.

Save transient screenshots and raw measurements only in ignored `.experiments/gate2-critic/round-N/`. Capture is diagnostic evidence and is not committed or delivered as a screenshot pile. Only hidden/headless local-browser review is authorized under the user’s correction. After round 3, automatic approval review rejected a builder’s local propulsion video because of the user’s video preference; subsequent critic review uses screenshot sequences and real keyboard snapshots only, with no new local video recording or playback. The round-1 video below is historical evidence, not permission to repeat it. A separate headless run without recording/screenshots reports the actual browser/GPU, DPR, frame percentiles and long frames at 1920×1080. Foreground desktop certification remains unverified and cannot be claimed from these measurements.

Each completed round records separate scores, pass/fail, a ranked issue list with reproduction/evidence, player impact, responsible module and measurable acceptance. Rerun affected scenarios and check regressions after fixes. Stop at first verified pass or 10 completed rounds; never reset the counter. The integrator owns status/roadmap updates.
