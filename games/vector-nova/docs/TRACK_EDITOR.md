# Track Workshop

Open the top-level **Editor** tab in the full game or browser build. The empty canvas and **File → New track** offer three playable starters with scenery and pickups already set up. **Waterfront loop** is a jump-free 3 km circuit, **Forest descent** is a jump-free 5 km downhill, and **Canyon jump practice** is a 5 km circuit with one jump. Choose **Draw from scratch** to place points on the 100 m grid. A starter creates a new draft in one undoable step. **Road → Create road** changes the road on the current draft. Choose a style and length, then select **Create road**. Woven requires Circuit. Downhill has a supported 600 m braking runout after the finish. Browser development also accepts `?editor=1` after career selection.

## Click, shape, drive

The 3D view is the editing surface. One compact authoring strip overlays its top-right corner. Three mode buttons stay visible and highlight the active mode: **Draw road**, **Select / move** and **Height**. Draw adds points; Select / move drags them along the ground; Height drags them vertically. D/V/H choose tools without affecting text fields; native buttons provide keyboard/controller access. **Point**, **Bank** and **Jumps** expand options inline in the same strip. Choosing an option applies it and collapses that group. Focus, Overview, Zoom in/out, Fly and Map stay directly available.

A ghost point and road continuation preview show where the next Draw click will go, including over existing road. Moving a point or using its height arrow while drawing keeps Draw active, so you can continue placing points afterward. Circuit closes automatically; after four or more points, choose **Finish drawing** or click the starting point. The editor frames your road and enters Select / move. Click a road point and drag it to bend the route; Shift-drag or its height arrow changes elevation. Hold Alt for finer height movement; the live guide reports the height delta. Native Raise/Lower, previous/next and section controls provide keyboard/controller alternatives. Double-click between handles in Select / move to insert a point. One Undo restores a complete drag.

Track checks identify the point behind a tight corner or other local problem. Click the **Point N · Tight corner** warning to select and zoom to the actual road point. A red halo marks the measured problem location in 3D and selects its nearby editable control; Map marks that control too. The checks report local 3D curve radius and grade. Bends below 55 m radius and climbs above 45% grade are amber recommendations: Test drive remains available. Authored road blocks only below max(35 m, half the road width + 4 m) radius or above 125% grade, alongside the existing spacing, frame/rotation, crossing and clearance checks. A crest/dip can need a gentler elevation transition. Seeded generation keeps the stricter 55 m / 45% profile. Separate links expose other problems. **Auto fix corner** opens an eligible bend by moving nearby controls or adding support points, keeping existing IDs/heights and distant road intact. It can optionally soften a warned corner while an independent climb or another corner remains. The footer distinguishes playable cautions from blocking errors; Test drive stays gated only by blocking errors. One Undo restores the complete edit. Protected jump/rotation areas or layouts needing larger changes require a manual edit. On a plain road without jumps or twists, **Spread road out** preserves the layout's relative shape and elevations while giving all bends more physical room. It makes the road longer and remains undoable; checks are never bypassed.

Select a section to highlight the part you are changing. **Bank / twist** gradually rotates the road surface by 90°, 180° or 270°; it does not turn the route's heading. Following points retain the exit angle so chunks connect, and the selected point shows its current road angle. Circuits automatically make a smooth return to the starting angle near the finish. Leave enough clear tail road for that return, or connect chunks into a complete turn. Straight aligns a span between its endpoints. Expand the selection when it needs more room. Point and Bank options provide precise coordinates, bank trim, width and section endpoints beside the mode buttons.

The **Jumps** group offers Small / Medium / Large presets. Select a road point and **Preview new jump here**, or **Find clear space** to search the course. A translucent proposal labels its takeoff and landing and reports the actual gap, receiver and total footprint. **Apply jump** commits one undoable edit; Cancel or Escape leaves the road unchanged. Anchors are sampled between existing points, so sparse roads do not need extra points first. The requested size is a horizontal guide; the final curved gap can be longer. Advanced endpoints remain available for custom spans and huge jumps.

New assisted jumps retain a bounded original-corridor snapshot. **Preview resize** restores that baseline before reshaping, avoiding accumulated ramp changes; Apply keeps the same jump ID. Editing the generated ramp manually requires rebuilding explicitly. Older/generated jumps use **Preview rebuild**, adopting their current road as the first baseline; their discarded historical road cannot be reconstructed. Cruise/boost forecasts use the selected stock ship and shared ballistic engine. They are reference conditions, not minimum-speed guarantees, and omit scenery collisions and steering. Structural road checks and flight advice are separate.

Jump lips stay anchored to road points while other sections change. Removing a gap restores the road but retains its ramp/landing point edits. Undo restores the complete previous edit. Generating replaces the route and its jumps; changing course type clears incompatible features. Both operations remain undoable.

Generate road offers **3, 5, 10, 15 and 20 km** presets with visible **Curviness**, **Crossovers**, **Vertical variation** (**Descent strength** for Downhill), **Jump frequency** and **Jump size** controls. Seeds choose different route layouts. Curviness makes ordinary turns broader at 0 and tighter at 100. Circuit seeds remain closed loops. New Downhill generation uses a sustained high-to-low profile across ordinary road as well as jump drops; stronger descent settings increase the fall. The starting grid and supported braking runout remain level. The header shows the actual net height drop of the resolved road.

For Circuit, **Crossovers** runs from **Broad loop** at 0 to **Intertwined** at 100. Zero preserves the existing broad-loop generation. Positive values create figure-eight or multi-crossing layouts with grade-separated overpasses; longer courses have room for more weaves. Required overpass height is automatic, even with Vertical variation at 0. Crossovers is disabled and ignored for Downhill. Changing these settings affects the next generation; saved road geometry is not silently rebuilt.

Jumps reserve actual approach, flight and receiving road. Broad-loop Normal frequency starts with one Circuit jump through 3 km and two beyond, or three Downhill jumps below 10 km and four above. Shorter or denser woven layouts may fit fewer weaves, fewer jumps or smaller jumps than requested; frequency and size always fit the available road and clearance. Downhill also reserves ordinary road for sustained descent. None creates a route without gaps. Pickup fill is enabled by default. **Generate road** repeats the chosen Road seed and settings; **New road variation** chooses a new Road seed and generates in one undoable action. New road variation keeps keyboard/controller focus for repeated rerolls. Requested downhill length includes its braking runout. New generation records generator version 4; versions 1–3 remain reproducible through their explicit historical builders. Saved geometry remains authoritative when loaded, so an existing downhill keeps its authored road until Generate road is chosen.

## Around your road

**Road** and **Environment** share the preview, course file and Undo/Redo history. On wide screens, the left scenery palette stays visible while you edit the road and gives its space to **Create road** or **Fill pickups** when opened. Choose **Environment** to inspect scenery with the road handles hidden or to open the palette on a narrower screen. Switching contexts preserves the road tool, selection and camera.

Environment offers all 17 base world families: **Nightweir, Halcyon, Asterion Orbital, Nacre Tempest, Vesper Forest, Glasswake Comet, Cindercoil, Saffron Leviathan, Zephyr Basilica, Helion Flare, Umbra Fold, Shatterline, Worldspire, Colossus Works, Ravel, Nomad Procession and Oriel**. Mode variants share their base family. Each family has curated character presets, a corresponding road kit and its registered sky/lighting. These are generated whole-course environments; route-specific campaign set pieces and active campaign hazards are not copied into custom courses.

Nightweir retains **Waterfront**, **Industrial basin** and **Reclaimed waterfront**. Its regional plan fills the course and surrounding space with connected terrain, rivers, basins, founded districts, quays, working yards and parks. New environments use the game’s production buildings, forest, ice, islands, shipyards, observatories, mineral formations and machinery as complete seeded assemblies. Nacre adds ocean shelves, reef habitats and tidal observatories; suspended worlds keep their floating foundations. Nightweir uses complete occupied civic and working-waterfront buildings. Required road edges, supports and foundations stay automatic. Dry-world horizons surround the surveyed terrain; local depressions and their foundations use the actual terrain height, without an overlapping flat ground layer. The editor does not require arranging individual buildings or plants. Most surrounding art is decorative; selected structures and tree trunks have contact, while the full visible assembly is kept clear of the road and jump corridors. Active campaign hazards remain future work.

**Road seed** determines the next route. **Environment seed** determines scenery independently. Pick a biome and a thumbnail swatch, then choose **Apply environment**. Fullness and seed are under **Scenery settings**. **Try another variation** picks another environment seed and applies it in one undoable action. Both preserve road points, angles, jump anchors and pickups. The applied scenery fits itself around the road as you draw or generate it. A blank draft shows a general preview of the biome. Save stores the applied world.

Pickups fills the course with real Vector boosts, Aegis shields, Repulsors, Arc bursts and Ion wakes. Balanced/Boost heavy and spacing options offer a few useful variations. Clear and Undo restore the expected layouts. Devices follow the actual banked/inverted road surface and avoid jump corridors. Refill after substantial road changes to update safe placement.

The header holds track name, course type, File, Save, Test Drive, Undo/Redo, Tunes and Back. **File** opens under its button and holds New track, saved tracks, Reset road, Import and Export. The right strip holds viewport tools and the Point, Road section and Jumps groups. Tunes stays clear of the strip and header controls. Tunes and Map close each other when opened.

## Camera, controls and testing

Drag the open 3D canvas to pan on the X/Z plane. Drag a handle to edit the track. In Draw mode, click to place a point and drag the open canvas to pan. Right-drag or Space-drag orbits, and the mouse wheel zooms. Middle-drag and Shift-right-drag also pan. New generation frames the complete layout. Loading long courses initially frames their first 1.5 km. Overview frames the entire route. Focus selection (or F) brings the selected point or section close enough to edit. Ordinary Undo/Redo keeps your camera in place. Restoring a replaced route reframes it. Map opens an interactive top-down popover over the 3D preview. Close it with its close button, an outside click, moving focus outside it, or Escape/B. Fly camera enables WASD and Q/E movement, drag look and Shift for speed. A standard controller uses left stick movement, right stick look and shoulder buttons for elevation. Escape/B leaves free camera and returns focus to its launcher.

Native header, drawer and authoring-strip controls provide keyboard/controller equivalents for editing gestures. The permanent camera controls offer Focus, Overview, Zoom in/out, Fly and Map. Mouse gestures handle orbit and pan; Fly provides keyboard/controller movement and look. Tab/Shift-Tab moves through visible controls and keeps focus in view; Enter/Space activates. Controller D-pad/stick moves through the same controls, A activates, and left/right adjusts native selectors/sliders. Escape/B leaves free camera or closes open controls first, then stops Draw and clears the current point or section selection without removing road points. A pending jump preview cancels before closing its group. A subsequent press while idle returns from the editor; holding Escape does not repeat those steps. The three authoring modes and camera controls remain visible while options expand or collapse. Ctrl/Cmd+Z undoes, Shift+Ctrl/Cmd+Z redoes, and Ctrl/Cmd+S saves. Physical Steam Input/device checks remain separate release validation.

Track checks explain incomplete routes, tight bends, steep grades, abrupt rotation, road/water clearance and landing-run issues before Test drive. Incomplete or invalid drafts can still be edited and saved. **Test drive** starts ordinary stock-ship Practice at the beginning of the course. Open **Test drive options** for **From selected point**, **This jump**, or **Retry last**. The jump test starts on the supported approach before takeoff. Choose entry speed and reserve in the Point or Jumps group. These settings describe the start of the test, and launch speed depends on how you drive. **Retry last** repeats the previous start settings on the same course geometry. R or Pause → Restart also retries the current drive with reset devices and physics. Pause → Back to editor restores the draft, selection and history. The last launch speed and landing outcome appear in the editor and the options menu, with a marker for a miss. Settings returns to the paused drive. Tests on the same course and ship reuse a suspended Practice session. Custom tracks do not earn campaign rewards or official Time Trial records. Any previous campaign session stays suspended.

## Saves and limits

Save stores the draft in My tracks, including empty drafts. Save a copy creates a new library ID. My tracks also offers Load, Delete saved, Import and Export. Leaving/reopening the editor in the same frontend retains the draft. Applied edits also checkpoint to a separate browser recovery slot after 700 ms and on page hide/exit. After refresh, **Continue draft** restores it, or **Start fresh** discards the checkpoint. Resolve that choice before authoring. Recovery never overwrites My tracks or career saves; explicit Save/Export remain available. Pending environment controls are not part of the applied recovery document. A storage failure keeps the previous checkpoint and reports the need to Save/Export. Import changes the draft and requires an explicit Save before changing the library. Failed writes and malformed files preserve the current document.

**Reset track** clears the road, jumps and pickups and returns to Draw. It retains the track name, identity and world settings. Undo restores the complete previous draft; Reset does not overwrite a saved track until you Save.

Browser builds use independent localStorage and file import/download. Electron uses atomic files in the full edition's `userData/tracks` directory and native `.vntrack.json` dialogs, separate from careers. Both accept the same track-file format, including all bundled biomes and assisted jump data. Libraries support 256 tracks and a 1 MB file limit. Steam Cloud/Workshop sharing is not configured.

Version-1 files store stable ID/name, a registered whole-course biome, road seed/options, independent versioned environment seed/layout, actual route points and unwrapped roll, world character/settings, anchored gaps and a resolved pickup layout. Legacy files without an environment block inherit their old seed and keep the previous roadside layout until explicit environment regeneration. New files use production scenery layout version 4. Saved layouts 0–3 keep their previous scenery until you explicitly regenerate the environment; Shatterline retains its production scenery introduced in layout 3. Regeneration upgrades the surroundings in one undoable action and preserves the authored road. Loading never regenerates saved road geometry. Unknown fields are dropped; unsupported versions/biomes/objects are rejected. Files contain no scripts, arbitrary asset URLs or filesystem paths.

One Circuit or Downhill course per file; up to 256 route points and 25 km of authored road. Generated lengths remain 1.8–20 km; the extra authoring room lets users reshape a 20 km starter without immediately invalidating it. Width is 32–64 m, elevation 0–3000 m, and bank trim about ±20°. Rotation chunks allow full inversions independently of trim. Branches, tubes, moving machinery, freeform wave curves and optional Freestyle trick scoring remain future work.

See [the editor roadmap](../track-editor-roadmap.md) for per-span mixed biomes, independent sky choices, optional props/hazards and later simulation features. The world selector still chooses the whole environment/backdrop. A bounded road-finish pilot in **Road section** uses the shared From/To points to apply Nightweir or Vesper surface materials. Endpoint IDs follow later road edits; Apply/Clear, Undo and file roundtrips preserve the road geometry and driving rules. Passive join bands mark finish boundaries. Jump/twist corridors are protected, and finish overrides must be cleared before deleting their endpoints. This pilot does not mix scenery or gameplay hazards; broader biome transitions remain on the roadmap.

## Verification and resource measurements

Run `node --test --test-concurrency=2 src/editor/*.test.js`, `npm run test:fast`, `npm run lint` and `npm run build -- --configLoader native`. Browser tools require an isolated `VN_URL` and launch muted by default. `editor-contexts-verify.mjs` checks independent seeds/history/save behavior; its `--water-clearance` branch checks water-preset validation and Undo. `editor-iteration-verify.mjs` (also `--responsive` and `--incomplete-recovery-only`) hand-draws a sparse course through real preview clicks, checks proposal/Apply/resize, recovery, finish spans and native local retries. `editor-spans-verify.mjs` checks both pilot directions, delayed texture readiness/reflections and cheap 3D dragging. `editor-biomes-verify.mjs` checks every family and legacy roundtrips, with a short `--responsive` branch for final touch coverage. `editor-tools-verify.mjs` and `editor-toolbar-verify.mjs` cover authoring, camera, Map and focus behavior. `VN_URL=… VN_TRACK=/path/to/course.vntrack.json node tools/editor-jump-camera-verify.mjs` observes native jump-camera handoffs from public point starts, current Downhill generation and exact Save/Undo; add `--responsive` only for final portrait coverage. `node --test tools/editor-camera.test.mjs` checks frame-rate/phase and lane continuity against the production camera rig.

`VN_URL=… node tools/editor-world-budget.mjs --layouts=1,2 --local-rebuild` compares the same Nightweir road/environment inputs and performs a native selected-point height edit. Add `--practice` with `VN_QUALITY=low|high` for matched warm Practice measurements. The fixtures use road seed 42, environment 7001, Waterfront fullness .82 and 20 km Crossovers 1. Texture estimates include owned shader maps.
