# Nomad Procession — the salt procession

An open 12.2 km downhill sprint with a supported 600 m runout, built in the existing Ravel worktree. Eleven colossal wheeled land carriers cross a white salt basin. The route winds through cargo halls, climbs exterior hulls, crosses roof decks and returns to lower holds, with ten mandatory unsupported transfers. The 50 m main road widens to 80 m at arrivals. Long supported stretches, moderate climbs and drops, and real gently upswept departure lips preserve racing speed between jumps. The Passing Window links a sustained exterior wall ride to the broad lower deck of an overtaking neighbor.

## Moving road and collision

The [route model](../../src/levels/nomad/track/model.js) owns nominal geometry, actual road coverage, carrier transforms and transfer windows. The [motion adapter](../../src/levels/nomad/motion.js) exposes `frameAtTime(distance, time)`, `surfaceAtTime(distance, lateral, time)` and `motionAt(distance, time)`. Nominal sampling remains available for asset placement and collision construction; rendering uses the player's interpolated `presentationTime`.

The world uses the convoy's uniformly translating reference frame. Salt travel and wheel rotation express the shared 16 m/s ground speed; each carrier also has real bounded lateral and longitudinal translation. Ordinary carriers use 16 m longitudinal and 5 m lateral amplitudes with 36–44 second periods. The overtaking pair uses opposing 28 m longitudinal amplitudes, 6 m lateral amplitude and a 38 second period. Vertical displacement follows the salt grade. These same pure equations drive visible hulls, roads, pickups, navigation paint and physics.

The [collision world](../../src/levels/nomad/collision.js) combines the exact running skins, guards, retained Blender hulls, end bogies, route load frames, receiving crown, suspended freight, service installations and salt plane. The added Blender assemblies use the same evaluated faces for rendering and native collision. Saltwind alone substitutes its locally opened hull and matching major hull collider; all other carriers retain the shared body. Carrier triangles retain named motion groups. [Dynamics](../../src/levels/nomad/dynamics.js) stages their continuous rigid translations in native Rapier; the authored moving surface chart remains authoritative for grounded support. Launches inherit material velocity and arrivals use relative velocity. Each [racer runtime](../../src/levels/nomad/runtime.js) owns its simulation clock, with snapshot/restore and reset preserving carrier phase. The [interception pilot](../../src/levels/nomad/pilot.js) steers through ordinary inputs toward the predicted moving arrival. It supplies no hidden launch impulse or midair correction.

## World layers

The retained Blender carrier kit supplies formed armor, recessed navy structure, open cargo portals, eighteen huge wheels, cranes, freight sockets and interior working lights. Separate load frames follow the exact hull-road guides and seat on actual exported hull triangles. A substantial formed cargo crown gives the overtaking receiver thick shoulders and a deep open arrival. [Working districts](../../src/levels/nomad/districts.js) consume the public Worldkit industrial kit to give the carriers distinct loading, hydraulic, cooling, freight-gallery, transmission and service roles. Fitted foundations, wall-mounted outriggers, pressure feeds and roof hangers connect those assemblies to the hull; lateral portals and the full road, camera and flight envelopes remain clear. Two [grounded companion carriers](../../src/levels/nomad/companions.js) reuse the same fleet kit and fitted service armor beyond the route; they remain visual convoy members, outside the eleven road owners, checkpoints, support and collision groups.

Freight Cathedral carries a roof-seated train of three staggered suspended loads. Crane Alley's [ceiling-hung hoist](../../src/levels/nomad/hall-machinery.js) exposes a bronze drum beneath open cast bearings, a shaft-aligned gearbox, port cooler and outboard service bridge; its packed render geometry also supplies exact native collision faces. Saltwind's [three recessed working bays](../../assets/levels/nomad/blender/saltwind-gallery/saltwind-gallery-provenance.json) pair horizontal cooling, an open maintenance gallery/hatch and a tall canted radiator. That export contains `nomad_saltwind_hull` and `nomad_saltwind_gallery`: the first replaces only Saltwind's body, while the second adds the fitted bay geometry at identity in the same carrier frame. The shared carrier source and wheel instances stay unchanged. The opened interior retains structural breaks, a profiled outer wall, roof and real cargo portals.

The [road](../../src/levels/nomad/track/road.js) uses warm working-steel plates, a graphite keel, warm-metal guards and steady inset navigation. [Guidance paint](../../src/levels/nomad/guidance.js) marks forward travel, departure warnings and arrivals on the actual moving skin. Canonical Worldkit steel/enamel finish remains attached to carrier-local or road coordinates. [Armor coating variation and salt wear](../../src/levels/nomad/armour-finish.js) add subtle local color and roughness without new imagery. [Quiet tire contacts](../../src/levels/nomad/wheel-contact.js) seat all main and companion wheels on the actual graded salt, and [low salt wakes](../../src/levels/nomad/dust.js) begin at wheel contacts. Fine salt cracks and grain filter out toward the horizon. The [generated cloud panorama](../../src/levels/nomad/backdrop.js) supplies distant sky radiance and reflections aligned with the warm daylight key. Nearby salt and vehicles remain geometry. Low quality preserves the convoy and essential guidance; reduced motion restrains decorative dust and freight without changing the moving-deck challenge.
