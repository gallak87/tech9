# 05 — Story personality and first-region design

**Type:** collaborative planning with the owner. **Status:** deferred until Kaida is accepted.

**Requires:** 03's playable Kaida. This discussion can overlap 04; no chapter implementation begins until both finish.

**Read:** [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md), the agreed direction in [DECISIONS.md](../DECISIONS.md), the original [concept](../../chronoforge/CONCEPT.md), and selected original visual/content references. Treat older story ideas as references, not a completed narrative bible.

## Purpose

The owner wants a real pass at story and personality before building the game around the finished character. The original 2D game has little developed story, and the prototype's story felt too wacky. The owner may drive the storyline directly.

Chrono Trigger was an inspiration for Chronoforge. Use that as context for discussing party chemistry, curiosity, warmth, memorable encounters, and emotional stakes. It does not prescribe copied characters, plot beats, or mandatory time travel. Ask which qualities the owner wants to carry forward.

## Work with the owner

Start with a focused conversation about the premise they want to tell, the crew's relationships, the desired balance of humor and seriousness, and what should matter emotionally in the opening region. Do not replace that conversation with an unsolicited finished campaign.

Offer a small number of concrete alternatives when helpful: a short opening situation, a crew interaction, or a sample dialogue exchange. Label them proposals. If the owner supplies a storyline, develop it rather than automatically pitching a new one.

Resolve:

- What Kaida wants personally, why Vex and Rune travel with her, and where their personalities complement or conflict.
- What has happened to this place, whom the crew cares about, and why reclamation matters beyond collecting resources.
- The first region's local problem, discovery, escalation, climax, and visible payoff at home.
- How an encounter, a useful reward, a skill/equipment choice, and one settlement change express that story through play.
- What belongs to established world identity and what remains open, including the treatment of the Void Architect and larger civilization arc.

Haventide/coastal reclamation is a proposed starting setting, not a locked story. Bellwether and the prototype's events are not inherited canon. Keep the wider world available without planning every biome upfront.

## Deliverables and done

Create a concise `STORY.md` with the owner's accepted premise, tone, character voices/relationships, first-region beats, and any still-open choices. Create or update `GAME_DESIGN.md` with the playable region's route, encounters, rewards, progression/settlement connections, and finish criteria. Keep the two documents linked without duplicating their content.

Use this design to turn 06 and 07 into appropriately sized implementation assignments. If the owner is absent, prepare a concise discussion brief and leave creative choices visibly open. Do not claim story acceptance, generate a whole narrative, or begin building the chapter automatically.
