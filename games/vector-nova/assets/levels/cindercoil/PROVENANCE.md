# Cindercoil original scene and geometry

The selected scene reference is [reference/cindercoil-scene-v1.png](reference/cindercoil-scene-v1.png), generated with the built-in `image_gen.imagegen` tool on 2026-09-29. The exact production prompt is preserved in [reference/prompt.txt](reference/prompt.txt). No uploaded image, external texture, borrowed mesh, or artist-specific style was used.

[build_cindercoil.py](build_cindercoil.py) translates the reference's layered basalt fault, three thick descending lava channels, ropy crust rafts, hollow columnar tower, and broken ivory/charcoal launch buttress into original Blender geometry. The editable source is `cindercoil-hero-kit.blend`; its preserved export is `public/assets/levels/cindercoil/cindercoil-hero-kit.glb`. Rebuild with Blender 5.1.1 in background mode using the generator; it uses CPU modeling/export and makes no renders. `asset-manifest.json` records mesh and triangle counts.

The kit uses bulging closed cross-section lava tubes with fractured cooling shoulders, unequal layered rock faces, and sheared asymmetric crust plates with broken folds. Rock groups retain their proportions and root into the sculpted terrain. Runtime material detail adds domain-warped multi-scale color and normal variation with the original basalt surface tile below; lake fissures use irregular cell boundaries. This is an authored lightweight approximation of the concept, not a photogrammetric reconstruction or fluid simulation. Runtime scenery adds stratified caldera terrain, instanced basalt and crust families, the selected cool ash panorama, a rough crusted lake shader, and modeled vent cores and bounded ballistic molten spray. The track owns the actual driving/launch surface; the asset's broken buttresses remain outside its flight envelope.

Wall opening, waterfall pulse, raft separation and vent state derive from the same deterministic level simulation time. No wall-clock animation, gameplay force or hidden collision is introduced here. Reduced motion removes ash drift and decorative lava oscillation while event phase remains accurate. High and low retain the same hero geometry, terrain and lighting; low reduces ash count. Blender sources and the generated reference are unserved working assets. Raw verification evidence remains ignored; this provenance records asset construction only.

The original game-owned bounded ash/ember fields, local heat veil and peripheral canvas are in `src/levels/cindercoil/atmosphere.js` and `overlay.js`. Their exposure/receiving approach is informed by the repository weather module and forest adapter; no weather source or external assets were copied. The selected sky has separate [provenance](SKY_PROVENANCE.md).

The v2 kit is generated by [build_cindercoil_v2.py](build_cindercoil_v2.py), retaining the original generator, .blend and export. `cindercoil-hero-kit-v2.blend` and `asset-manifest-v2.json` preserve that export. It adds low slumped cooling-flow aprons and fractured edge bevels.

[basalt-albedo-v1.png](basalt-albedo-v1.png) is an original diffuse surface tile generated with built-in imagegen on 2026-09-30, without reference images or external art. The [exact prompt](basalt-prompt-v1.txt) is preserved. Its unmodified runtime copy is triplanar mapped onto the authored terrain/rock; shader-derived relief is an approximation rather than a measured normal map. No photograph is used as nearby scenery.

The current runtime kit is `public/assets/levels/cindercoil/cindercoil-hero-kit-v3.glb`, generated by [build_cindercoil_v3.py](build_cindercoil_v3.py) with editable `cindercoil-hero-kit-v3.blend` and `asset-manifest-v3.json`. It keeps the v2 rock families and refines lava-channel thickness, cooling shoulders and embedded crust. Original and v2 sources/exports remain unchanged.
