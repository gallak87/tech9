# Cindercoil — road finish

Use the [road workflow](README.md). Target `cindercoil`; preserve heat and spill readability.

## Starting point

The [production road](../../../src/levels/cindercoil/track/production.js) combines beveled construction with [licensed mineral PBR maps](../../../src/levels/cindercoil/track/ceramic-maps.js) and independent [fitted seam shading](../../../src/levels/cindercoil/track/surface-finish.js). Pale, charcoal and oxide ceramics are mostly matte; Crown panels use a distinct darker glazed finish. The same construction can supply physical solids.

## Investigate

Check whether the surface conveys dense fired ceramic and formed plate edges at racing distance. Examine texture scale, bump response and the separation between matte bodies and glazed panels in both cool light and reflected lava warmth. Richer surface response may help, but glossy treatment across every tile would lose the existing material hierarchy. Choose the intervention from actual views, not a requirement for more cracks or soot.

## Keep and check

Compare an ordinary ceramic connector, the glazed Crown, the furnace roll and Curtain Reach; include the vent landing and a lava/spill boundary across the road beside the protected lane. Match active and quiet surge phases in before/after views; the diagonal crater spill remains continuously active. Reflected lava warmth must not make safe road resemble an active spill or hide the true hazard edge. Keep tile relief stable under moving highlights and ensure warm reflections retain detail. Preserve [spill openings and contact intervals](../../../src/levels/cindercoil/spill.js), warned surge boundaries, collision collection and supported road profiles. Run affected ceramic/road or spill checks alongside the shared runtime check. Shape and collision-only construction must remain independent of texture loading.

## Material source

[Concrete030 from ambientCG](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Concrete030) is CC0 procedural material data, adapted to a neutral fired substrate. [Sources, build and provenance](../../../assets/levels/cindercoil/ceramic/) retain the exact inputs and conversion. Grain repeats every 3 m independently of the original 6 × 12 m cassettes. Road bodies share the three compressed maps; construction joints use filtered analytical relief without displacement. Ordinary ivory/oxide remains matte; the Crown has a restrained dark glaze. The existing district probe is borrowed, and shape/collision construction loads no maps.
