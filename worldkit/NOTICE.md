- New Living Forest bark and cutout foliage images: [generated-art provenance](modules/living-forest/assets/provenance.json), including prompt and byte checksum. Living Forest geometry is newly authored procedural art.

- Terrain PBR maps: [texture sources](modules/landscape/assets/runtime/textures/SOURCES.md).
