# Saffron Leviathan assets

`scene-reference.png` was generated with the built-in imagegen tool on 2026-09-29 for this level. It is the selected modeling reference, not a runtime background. No external asset packs or borrowed textures are used.

Exact generation prompt: “Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: 3D game scene concept reference for Blender modeling, landscape wide. Build Saffron Leviathan: archaeological alien desert at impossible scale, pale stratified bone fossil with a truly traversable huge skull mouth and open interior, mismatched broken vertebrae forming separated platforms, a living segmented burrower breaching far alongside the ancient fossil. A 32-metre wide thick smoked-gold glass racing ribbon descends into skull mouth, banks visibly inside with bright exit skylights, then jumps across broken vertebrae. Tiny sleek hover racing craft for scale, no wheels. Foreground split low horizontal ceramic baffles, two vanes with shadow gaps, thick transparent amber glass shoulders. Layered saffron dunes and slow sandfalls pouring through porous eroded ribs. Burrower is a distinct living tapered plated animal with hooked head, not a repeating tube. Immense dry lavender-hazed sky, pale hard sun and long shadows, warm sand-bounce readable skull interior. Sculptural restrained premium game world design, aged eroded bone plates and holes, large/medium/small anatomy, no neon road, no flat yellow background, no human architecture except a small suspended survey station, no text or watermark. Show a clear driving-camera composition that explains skull passage and road/landing clearance.”

`build_saffron.py` is the reproducible editable Blender source; `leviathan-kit.blend` is its saved Blender 5.1.1 scene. Run Blender in background with the script to export `public/assets/levels/saffron/leviathan-kit.glb`. Modeling and export used CPU only. The kit includes the skull shell with actual orbital/skylight holes, mandibles and teeth, three vertebral variants with joined cortical processes and shallow geometric erosion cups, three eroded rib variants, two displaced dune families, two stratified eroded shelves, a bone-shelf sandfall with six irregular sheets spread through depth, and a plated burrower with jaws and protected eyes. Overlapping armor follows the animal’s cross section, with baked depth colors at overlaps. Materials are authored PBR solids with level-owned filtered mineral/porosity shading. The current skull has irregular thick orbital apertures and 92 seeded real cortical pores with rounded geometry rims and baked cranial shelter colors; ribs retain curved irregular cortical fragments. The original anatomy and generator remain editable. Landscape shading, sand boundary motion and creature animation are authored in the level.

Approximation: surface porosity is represented by real large openings, mineral seams and layered anatomy rather than dense sculpted microcavities. The burrower uses deterministic world transforms and segment motion rather than a biological simulation. Sandfall surfaces and grains complement fixed geological meshes.

Module origins, exported bounds, polygon counts and source/runtime SHA-256 hashes are in `asset-manifest.json`. All authored files are project-owned; no third-party attribution or license dependency was introduced.

The selected lavender panorama is exported unchanged to `public/assets/levels/saffron/saffron-sky-v2.png`; its prompt and sources remain in [sky provenance](SKY_PROVENANCE.md). Sky projection overlaps the longitude seam and blends the poles, with a single source moon and separately authored scene lighting.

The original sand/grain shaders use the project weather module and forest adapter as architectural references for bounded world-space layers, shelter and coordinated exposure. No source code, textures or bootstrap from those references is copied. Road-relative skull cover replaces their static X/Z surface field.
