# Glasswake scene assets

- [Exact original generation prompt](scene-prompt.txt).
- `scene-reference.png`: original built-in OpenAI image generation, created for this level in this task. Prompt direction: cobalt comet ravines, pale fractured ice, a supported transparent crystal raceway with real fracture jumps, warm blade-fin fittings, a calving shelf exposing a luminous cavern, and violet-green aurora. Reference only; no image pixels load in the game.
- `generate-environment.py`: original procedural Blender authoring script based on that selected scene composition, created in this task. Run `/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender --background --factory-startup --python assets/levels/glasswake/generate-environment.py` from the game root. Geometry/export were generated on Blender 5.1.1 using CPU background mode; no GPU render or bake was performed.
- `glasswake-environment.blend`: editable native Blender scene with named prototype meshes in metre units. Templates deliberately overlap at the origin for route placement. Includes three broad stratified cliff families with asymmetric contours and melt pockets, rubble shelf, dark basal strata, distant fragment, thick open barrel-vault cavern roof/pier, continuous scalloped ice curtain, rounded hollow bell vault, dark trapped inclusion, engineered crystal arch, thick landing shelf, dock mast, a fixed layered cavern and fifteen independent calving pieces.
- `public/assets/levels/glasswake/glasswake-environment.glb`: runtime export of that scene; 48 authored meshes, 42,284 source triangles, 2,769,136 bytes, no textures. Runtime instancing places these forms using track frames. No external assets or third-party licences are involved.
- The calving event uses deterministic absolute route progress. Branching seams propagate before fifteen independently posed chunks and chips fall away from the actual back wall, floor and ledges. Both quality presets and reduced motion preserve timing, event boundaries and geometry. This is an authored scenery animation, not a physical ice simulation.
- Broad material response is authored with opaque roughness/metalness and pale top crust; the route owns optically clear deck materials. Shadowed cavern lighting blends the borrowed scene key/fill and adds three local road practical lights plus one non-shadow cyan practical inside the calving cavern; disposal restores the scene's previous light intensities, environment intensity and fog.

The first production stills informed denser coherent fracture contours, sculpted top crust, smooth eroded vaults and curtains, and a dark continuous basin floor. Spatially partitioned instance batches allow camera and shadow culling. Geology casts shadows onto the road but does not receive unstable glancing self-shadows. Adjacent interior sections share one smooth light transition.

The cavity practical ramps from zero with the same deterministic reveal state used by the moving shelf. Its light reaches the authored rear wall, ledges and falling fragments; high/low/reduced-motion settings share the same illumination state. It adds no shadow or reflection render pass.

Thawlight Return adds a bounded amber-white tint to borrowed key/fill colors, with smooth 110 m fades into and out of the section. Every update derives from saved baseline colors; reset, cavern entry, other sections and disposal restore those colors exactly. Ambient colors and light direction remain unchanged.

The original scene reference, prompt, generator, Blender scene and export remain unchanged. `refine-environment.py` opens that scene and writes `glasswake-environment-v2.blend` and its runtime GLB: 49 templates, 43,644 triangles, 2,924,316 bytes. It adds torn asymmetric curtain sheets, eroded vault ends, coherently tilted cliff crowns and a closed, recessed cavern wall family. Local material code adds metre-scaled fracture, strata, roughness and filtered relief; no image texture is used on near geology.

`aurora-sky-v1.png` is original built-in OpenAI image generation for this level, with no reference-image input. The exact prompt is in `sky-prompt.txt`. Actual selected output is 1774×887 pixels; the prompt requested a larger ideal size, which the tool did not return. The untouched source image is retained here and copied into the runtime asset directory. It supplies only sky/atmospheric radiance; all nearby terrain, supports and spectacle remain geometry. A source-native equirectangular shader overlaps the longitude ends with continuous sampling derivatives and restrains lower-hemisphere radiance. High/low use the same panorama; reduced motion freezes its slow drift. The backdrop owns and disposes its texture.

The calving shelf is staged 820 m ahead of its original bridge-axis position, retaining deterministic crack/separation timing and the original 15 moving parts. This keeps the cavity in the forward view through Fracture Reach and departure. Its actual transformed geometry remains covered by the route/flight/camera envelope check.

Thick recessed backing masses now join the Gallery curtains and the outer Chasm wall, with a deliberate open inner side in Chasm. A window in the Gallery foreground geology exposes the original dark meteor inclusion. A fixed non-shadow aperture spot grazes that inclusion and its ice bands; it owns its target and adds no shadow/reflection pass. Cavern ambient radiance is reduced to distinguish the recesses from the pale foreground. The three route practicals retain restrained illumination of the driving surface. All placements retain sampled route/camera clearance, and exterior stretches combine longer overlapping cliffs with eroded crowns.
