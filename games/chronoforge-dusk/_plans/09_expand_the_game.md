# 09 — Expand one region at a time

**Type:** future planning and repeated regional delivery. **Status:** outline only.

**Requires:** a finished first region and an owner decision to extend the game.

## Outcome

Grow the game toward the owner's chosen story arc and scale using the established asset, runtime, and playtest workflow.

For each next region:

1. Decide with the owner what story development, new place, and gameplay idea justify it.
2. Select useful references from original Chronoforge: Haventide, Emberline, Orbital Reach, Last Crown, Forest Veil, Mire Bog, Crater Ember, Frost Canyon, and their associated enemies/items/buildings are a content horizon, not an obligation to recreate every entry.
3. Plan a compact regional loop and its relationship to existing progression and home development. Introduce selected civilization-tier advances when earned by the wider game.
4. Identify new asset needs and reuse established Dusk assets/systems where appropriate. Preserve the original art identity without forcing every region into the same composition.
5. Deliver, integrate, and playtest this region before committing production effort to the next. Carry forward saves and verify progression through the current Dusk game.

Refine this outline into concrete assignments only when the next region is chosen. Do not generate an eight-biome master implementation spec now. The eventual game can be smaller or larger as the owner decides.

The future tech9 capability/director discussion can use evidence from these results, but it remains separately authorized work. Do not archive existing projects or redesign the framework as a side effect of regional development.

## Done and next

Repeat until the agreed story and game scope are present. Then use [10](10_finish_and_package.md) for whole-game completion, rather than treating a collection of individually tested regions as a proven complete playthrough.
