# Sound effects

Open the game with `?dev=1` or `?dev=2` to enable **Sounds**. It docks into the Ship inspector tools; wide menus also show it beside Tunes. On narrow screens, audition through the inspector to keep menu navigation clear. Pick a sound and press **Play**; picking another stops the current preview. Use **M** to pause Tunes while listening. Effects volume comes from Settings. Each preview stops automatically; engine and sustained effects play for three seconds.

These 33 choices are sorted alphabetically by group and name. Shared recordings appear once; volume-only variants and silent events have no separate entries. The Sounds panel uses the same generated samples, synthesis and effects volume as gameplay. It sets the output level before the first transient, keeps each decoded tail intact, and reports a missing sample instead of auditioning a substitute. The preview owns separate audio resources and does not activate pickups or alter the race. Audition from a menu or pause screen to hear the selected effect in isolation: racing audio and Tunes otherwise continue, and the isolated cue does not reproduce competition between voices in the full racing mix.

For pickup sound and VFX together, open **Ship inspector → Powerups & equipment** with `dev=1` or `dev=2`, or go directly to `?inspect=1&dev=1&ship=kestrel`. Choose a device and press **Trigger**. The Arc, Repulsor and Aegis sound sets match gameplay. Aegis has separate full-duration and absorb/break sequences. Check **Shield target** to preview a blocked first hit, and **Engine sound** to hear Driving/Boost alongside the device.

The cup reward starts at least 0.6 seconds into its reveal and 0.25 seconds after any remaining race-finish voice. It follows effects volume, cancels when leaving results, and does not replay when reopening saved standings. Reduced motion keeps the trophy visible without the entrance animation.

The propulsion bed continuously responds to acceleration, braking, steering and speed; its three presets are representative states, not separate recordings. A shielded `combat-hit` is silent because `shield-hit` supplies the audible cue. Arc, Repulsor and Ion blocked-hit confirmations share the Aegis collapse recording. Expiry, cancellation and attacker confirmation use quieter gains in gameplay; the inspector auditions those event sequences.

Arc and Repulsor finish through their existing tails, with no extra expiry cue. Aegis has no sustained audio layer; its start and collapse leave room for propulsion.

All 21 WAVs in `assets/audio/sfx/` are live: seven road-device cues, seven equipped-ability cues, one physical ship-impact cue, five lap/finish announcements and one championship reward. The seven equipment cues are generated first passes awaiting listening approval; their configs and exact trim/gain/fade edits are retained in `assets/audio/sfx/equipment-provenance.json`. Arc hull impact and target contact share `arc-hit.wav`. Aegis and Repulsor use the approved softened recordings. The race bus shares each announcement recording with Sounds, including lap/final lap and all three finish variants. Countdown/GO retain their original synthesized tones, levels and timing. Lap/finish announcements stay clear of the underwater filter; physical collisions use `ship-impact.wav` through the world mix. A shielded collision uses the Aegis block alone.

Original synthesis remains live for Vector activation/rush, Ion activation/wake/impact/confirmation, the approved Arc shots, propulsion and countdown/GO. Sample-backed cues have one source only; missing files stay silent and dev previews report the error. Unavailable pickups and Vector cancellation are silent, retaining HUD feedback. Ion emission ends by fading its bed over a few hundred milliseconds; there is no separate expiry chime. Ordinary Vector exit ends its rush without a separate cue. Aegis expiry/cancellation retain their warp collapse.

Environment sound work is deferred; the circuits retain their original thunder and shelf synthesis, with no environment entries in this picker.

Keep the catalog and this table aligned when a sound changes.

Equipped abilities share their samples between races, Sounds and the inspector. Lance retains the existing heavy projectile and Repulsor-class gameplay consequence, with equipment metadata choosing its distinct fire/impact recordings. Shielded hull hits stay silent; outgoing blocked shots and Deflector blocks use the established shield collapse. Deflector natural expiry uses a quieter collapse. Other equipment expiry/cancellation, unavailable activation and cooldown state are intentionally silent. The inspector previews every ability, plus separate Deflector expiry/block sequences; utility previews stage launch/landing, reserve refill, a four-second overtake and airborne stabilization using the shared effects. These isolated demonstrations do not run race physics or change career state. Jump’s quiet landing catch follows supported equipment-jump landings; recovery and ordinary course jumps retain their existing audio.
