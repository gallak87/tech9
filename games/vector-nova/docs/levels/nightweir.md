# Nightweir — Reservoir to Harbor

The user selected the [stormwater city concept](../../assets/levels/nightweir/reference/nightweir-v1.png), then authorized a separate Nightweir polish pass with the same downhill taste and an independent art gauntlet. Worldspire review remains separate.

The race descends roughly 3 km over 12.4 km of wet municipal roads, with eight physical jumps, three corkscrews, six falling-water crossings and a 650 m supported braking runout (13.0 km total). Banked reversals dominate; straight roads are reserved for short launch/receiving windows and the post-finish quay.

Occupied terraces, open gallery arches, deep service entries, turbines and framed feeder gates lead to a working harbor with sawtooth receiving halls and moored cargo barges. Replaceable sluice panels, connected hoist frames, stiffeners and worn contacts make the moving machinery legible. The road reuses Halcyon's licensed CC0 asphalt scan at its 2.05 m repeat, with local moisture and lighting. Open storm gutters, cast-iron grates and red spillway indicators provide the map-specific edge. Concrete uses Helion's licensed 4.4 × 2.2 m substrate with local formwork, rain and waterline staining. Shielded lamps reveal inhabited recesses and gate assemblies; broad metal highlights avoid distant sparkle. Editable Blender city geometry and matching solids are generated against the production route. The runtime city is a losslessly gzip-packed GLB, loaded through native streaming decompression; its unpacked hash is retained in model provenance.

The Floodgates mutation lifts sluice leaves during the approach and increases source-fed water flow. Gate state follows race distance; flow animation follows simulation time and freezes under reduced motion. Reused Nacre analytic waves supply sheltered basin presentation; Zephyr's curtain wetness field supplies a bounded 14% grip reduction and 0.8 drag only under crossings. A level-owned no-op world pass keeps transparent water in the linear HDR composition path at both quality settings, without enabling AO or bloom on Low. This is not general fluid-force simulation.
