## Lessons to carry into both lanes

Human review catches identity failures missed by high formal scores. Judge ordinary stretches alongside hero views; sky/water, road material, barriers, supports and surroundings must agree. Strong surface coverage and structural changes can communicate more than small neon highlights. Real gaps worked because they revealed the surrounding world; a real jump now needs equally honest motion and failure. Vary districts without losing one coherent level identity, preserve successful choices, show shape/feel early, and inspect real movement at high/low quality before claiming finished art. Neither added features nor a beautiful still compensates for unreadable driving or a weak remainder of the lap.
