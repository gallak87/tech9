Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: production concept for a 3D futuristic anti-gravity racing game, intended as reference for actual Blender models
Primary request: a coherent monumental solar-observatory race facility built beside the visible limb of a star. Wide landscape scene at driver's near-road perspective with foreground, middle distance and immense far depth. The broad 30-metre racing road is made of physically thick ivory ceramic heat-tile slabs with graphite undersides and overlapping sculpted copper feather radiator edges; road is readable and textured, not asphalt. A tiny angular silver racing craft gives scale. On one side a colossal curved charcoal sunshield has broad layered beveled plates, immense copper load spines and white thermal blankets at the roots; behind it a covered jump bridge has a thick cantilevered shield canopy with two split fins and visible landing deck. Across the middle distance stand several immense articulated feathered copper radiator fans with distinct joint angles, anchored to truss towers. A single complex precision instrument tower has stacked pale rings, dark sensor arms, glass control gallery and copper thermal mantles. Above the scene the star is a structured deep amber-red photosphere with cellular convection and darker filaments, showing its curved limb; it must not be a featureless white disk. Two gigantic prominences are distinct: a tall hooked branching plasma tongue breaks against the far side of the sunshield, and a lower broad double-crested plasma sheet curls around another distant shield. Exposed ceramic road warm in stellar light; shielded region cool and dark with purposeful amber service fill, never crushed black. Subtle luminous spill at shield edges; no blinding bloom. Hard-surface production game art, believable working thermal engineering, strong volumes and silhouettes, scene legible at driving speed. No generic orbital U frames, no thin orange trim, no city skyline, no text, no logos, no HUD, no watermark.
